# D35 — China Northeast: Ice, Rime and the Frozen Crater of Changbai

Researched 28 Nov 2026 (participant proposal). Machine-readable record: `data/destinations/D35-china-northeast.json`.

## Overview

A 13-day winter route through Heilongjiang and Jilin (Manchuria): Harbin (Russian-founded railway city, Ice and Snow World, Siberian Tiger Park, Sun Island), China Snow Town (Xuexiang), Jilin City and Rime Island (wusong rime ice on the Songhua), the North Slope of Changbai Mountain with the frozen Tianchi crater lake and hot springs, and Yanji in the Yanbian Korean Autonomous Prefecture. Window 13-25 Feb 2027, open-jaw CDG to Harbin and Yanji back via Beijing. No camping, no self-drive.

## Screening verdict

- **Seasonality:** Good in February (ice park, deep snow, rime, frozen Tianchi at -8/-21 C in Harbin; S10), fair in March (about 2/-11 C, ice park closed, rime unlikely).
- **Flights:** CDG-HRB needs a connection (Beijing or another hub). Planning estimate EUR 950 realistic (confidence: estimate; aggregator pages show wide, partly old ranges, S19). Spring Festival return flows push prices up.
- **Local costs:** Moderate (about EUR 1,220 per person pp before contingency).
- **Security:** Eligible (see below), with general detention/exit-ban cautions.
- **Cultural interest:** Russian-era Harbin, Manchu villages, Korean-Chinese Yanbian.
- **Adventure:** Cold endurance rather than technical; demanding physically because of -10 to -30 C.
- **Independent-travel feasibility:** Good by rail and bus with a few hired drivers; language, VPN and payments are the friction points.

## February vs March

February: Ice and Snow World (expected about 20 Dec 2026 to late Feb; last season 21 Feb 2026, S09), rime ice (late Dec to late Feb, S12), deep snow at Snow Town (S11), frozen Tianchi (S13). Spring Festival: Lunar New Year Sat 6 Feb 2027; public holiday 5-13 Feb (S15). Chunyun starts about 15 days earlier and return flows run past the holiday. The plan starts 13 Feb so Harbin is visited 15-17 Feb, before the likely park closure. The Lantern Festival is about 20 Feb (estimate).
March: Ice park closed, temperatures near freezing, melt on rivers and roads; cheaper flights and hotels. Not recommended for this concept.

## Itinerary rationale

Harbin first because the ice park may close in the last days of February. Snow Town by the winter shuttle (about 5.5 h, CNY 120; S11, S16) with two nights. Rail to Jilin, rime ice at dawn in Jilin and Rime Island (ferry, CNY 60; S12), then Changbaishan station (opened Dec 2021, S14) and Erdaobaihe for the North Slope (CNY 215 + 80; S13). End in Yanji for Korean food and fly from Yanji. Flexible days: 7, 11, 12.

## Transport and driving legality

Foreigners need a Chinese licence to drive; IDP not recognised (S02). Rail tickets are bookable on 12306 with a passport, but foreign-card payment may fail; Alipay or third-party sites are the fallback (S17). Hired drivers cover the rural legs.

## Security assessment (read 28 Nov 2026)

- France Diplomatie: mainland China normal vigilance (green) except Xinjiang; vigilance section dated 5 Mar 2026; alerts page 16 Nov 2026 lists El Nino, typhoons, Tibet (Gyirong), measles; nothing for Heilongjiang or Jilin (S05, S06). Border advice: do not photograph the North Korea border where prohibited, avoid boats offering to approach North Korean territory.
- UK FCDO: no against-travel wording; regional risks list only Tibet, Xinjiang, Myanmar border (S01, S04); safety page: detention under national-security laws, exit bans, terrorism cannot be ruled out (S02).
- US State Department: Level 2, Exercise Increased Caution, issued 18 Oct 2026 (arbitrary enforcement, exit bans, wrongful detention), read via the data feed (S07).
- Netherlands Worldwide and Smartraveller: not assessed; fetches failed or timed out.
Classification: **eligible**. The route stays away from North Korea border crossings. Reassess before booking and two weeks before departure.

## Signature experiences

Ice and Snow World after dark; dawn rime ice at Rime Island; frozen Tianchi from the North Slope; two nights at China Snow Town.

## Alternatives considered

- Mohe / Arctic Village (northernmost China): overnight train from Harbin about 16 h or a 1.5-2 h flight; about -30 C (S18). Needs 3-4 extra days; excluded to keep 13 days.
- Hulunbuir grassland and Manzhouli: not researched in depth; adds a long rail leg and fewer set pieces; not included.
- Yabuli ski resort: possible add-on near Snow Town road.
