# D39 — Indonesia — Papua, Raja Ampat Without the Highlands

Researched 28 November 2026. Participant proposal. Security status: **conditional**. Realistic total about EUR 2,314 per person (exceptional band), best month February.

## Overview
A 13-day boat-and-homestay route in the northern Raja Ampat islands (Southwest Papua) reached through Jakarta and Sorong. Highland, Central, South and the main Papua provinces are not visited. The route: Jakarta (buffer night), Sorong, ferry to Waisai, homestays at Sawinggrai (Gam), Kri and Arborek, Piaynemo by charter, two buffer days, ferry back, Jakarta, home.

## Screening verdict
- **Seasonality:** Feb–Mar fall in the October–April calmer-sea period (several guides; none gives a 2027 forecast). Showers all year. Manta timing differs by source.
- **Flights:** estimate only: about EUR 880 Paris–Jakarta plus about EUR 330 Jakarta–Sorong return. No live fare seen.
- **Local costs:** low per night (homestays with meals about IDR 350–500k, about EUR 17–25), but boat charters and 2026 entry fees (IDR 1.7–2.0M, about EUR 84–99) add up.
- **Security:** conditional (see below).
- **Cultural interest:** village homestays, crafts, a bird-guiding community; limited sites in the usual sense.
- **Adventure:** snorkelling, boat days, dawn walk; no technical skills.
- **Independent-travel feasibility:** workable through homestays and ferries; the dependence on weather and boat quotes is high.

## February vs March
February is preferred: Eid al-Fitr (about 9–10 March 2027) brings domestic travel peaks and unreliable ferries and flights, and Ramadan (about 8 Feb–9 Mar) overlaps both months. Late March after Eid is a workable alternative, not tested here.

## Itinerary rationale
Five homestay nights keep money in villages and give jetty snorkelling without a boat each day. Days 8 and 9 are weather buffers, and the Jakarta night before the return is a buffer against domestic delays. Wayag is optional because it needs a long boat day (3.5–4 h each way from Waisai).

## Transport and driving legality
No driving. Sorong–Waisai ferry (Express Bahari 09:00/14:00, about 2 h, IDR 215,000 economy), charter speedboats between islands, taxis in cities. No motorcycles.

## Security assessment (checked 28 Nov 2026)
- **France Diplomatie:** Papua, Central Papua, Highland Papua, South Papua "déconseillées aux voyageurs, sauf raison impérative" (Papua item updated 10 Sep 2026). West/Southwest Papua not named. 20 Aug 2026 attack and hostage-taking in Central Papua.
- **UK FCDO** (GOV.UK content API, 18 Sep 2026): no against-travel wording for any Papuan province found; detailed warning on separatist violence, Nduga 2023 kidnapping, foreigners declared targets, attacks on small aircraft, limited hospitals, evacuation to Darwin.
- **Netherlands Worldwide** (20 Oct 2026): orange for Papua, Highland, Central and South Papua; yellow elsewhere including West and Southwest Papua.
- **Smartraveller** and **US State Department:** could not be read directly (timeout; HTTP 403). Search extracts: reconsider travel to Papua Pegunungan and Papua Tengah (AU); Level 4 for Central and Highland Papua, Level 2 overall (US, copy dated 30 Apr 2025). Must be re-read.
- **Incidents:** 9 Oct 2026 firefight in Maybrat (Sorong hinterland); 2026 killings of civil servants in the Highlands (Sept) and other attacks; none found in Raja Ampat.
- **Verdict:** the route avoids every advised-against province, but the Papua region as a whole is in conflict and two advisories are unverified, so **conditional**. The Baliem Valley is left out because of the French and Dutch advice, the US Level 4 and the September 2026 killings in Jayawijaya. Cenderawasih Bay (Nabire, Central Papua) is left out for the same reason. The Arfak Mountains are not in an advised-against province but are left out as a precaution (neighbouring regencies with armed-group activity, extra flight). Triton Bay (Kaimana) was not researched in depth.

## Signature experiences
Jetty snorkelling at Arborek and Kri; red birds-of-paradise at dawn from Sawinggrai; Piaynemo by charter; two nights in an Arborek homestay.

## Alternatives considered
- Baliem Valley (Dani culture, Jiwika mummies): rejected, security.
- Arfak Mountains birding: rejected as precaution.
- Liveaboard: far above budget (from USD 5,795 per person quoted by one operator).
- Cheaper variant: Makassar–Sorong flights and public ferry (economical scenario, about EUR 1,722).

## Open questions and reassessment triggers
Re-read all five advisories within two weeks of booking; extension of any advisory to Southwest Papua or Sorong triggers exclusion; get real fares and two boat quotes; confirm surat jalan needs with a homestay; confirm the entry-fee total at SIPARI; see a travel clinic for malaria (Papua is a risk area).
