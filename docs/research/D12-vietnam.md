# D12 — Vietnam: The Far North on the Karst Roof

Research notes, verified 2026-11-27. Agent A12. Companion data: `data/destinations/D12-vietnam.json`.

## 1. Overview

Thirteen days in Vietnam's far-north limestone provinces, by private car with driver: Hanoi, Ba Bể lake, Cao Bằng and the Bản Giốc waterfall on the Chinese frontier, the Bảo Lạc mountain road, Mèo Vạc's Sunday market, Mã Pí Lèng pass and the Nho Quế gorge, the Đồng Văn Karst Plateau (UNESCO Global Geopark), Lũng Cú, Du Già and Quản Bạ, then back to Hanoi. Nights are in Tày stilt houses and Lô Lô, Hmong and Dao village homestays. The route avoids the classic north–south circuit (no Hạ Long, Hội An, Huế or Saigon, and no Sa Pa town).

Provincial note: after the June 2025 merger Hà Giang belongs to Tuyên Quang province, Bắc Kạn (Ba Bể) to Thái Nguyên, and Mù Cang Chải's Yên Bái to Lào Cai; Cao Bằng is unchanged (S24). Signs and operators still use the old names.

Window: **8–20 March 2027**. Day 1 Mon 8 Mar (depart), Day 2 arrive Hanoi, Day 7 = Sunday 14 March (Mèo Vạc market), Day 12 Fri 19 Mar (reserve day and overnight flight), Day 13 Sat 20 Mar (arrive Paris).

## 3. February vs March

- **Tết**: Saturday 6 February 2027; public-sector break reported as 4–10 February (S11); other sources show 5–9 or 5–11. Expect closures, higher prices, full buses and homestays for 1–2 weeks around it. Some operators surcharge.
- **February**: cold (day about 13–19 °C in Hà Giang town, colder on the plateau), fog in valleys and on passes until mid-morning, plum and peach blossom at peak, often bright clear afternoons (S14). Fog on passes is a recognised hazard.
- **March**: milder (day about 17–23 °C, night 7–12), clearer, drier to lightly showery (S15). Blossom fading. Rainfall figures vary between sources (30–80 mm), so they are given as ranges.
- **Rice terraces**: not part of the route. Mù Cang Chải and Y Tý terraces are bare in March (flooded stage and harvest are later in the year; general pattern, not verified against a source here). Y Tý cloud season is October–March but the road is rough and trekking infrastructure is undeveloped (search results); judged too far for this plan.
- **Hanoi air quality**: cool-season pollution is typical (general pattern, unverified live). Only about three nights in Hanoi.
- **Ramadan**: not relevant. **Buckwheat flowers** (Oct–Nov): not relevant.
- **Recommendation**: March. A February variant is possible after about 15 February.

## 5. Transport and driving legality

- **Law**: FCDO says drivers need a 1968 IDP and the national licence, for cars and motorbikes (S02). Vietnam accepts only the 1968 Vienna-convention IDP; 1949 Geneva IDPs are not valid, and the IDP must accompany a home licence that carries the motorcycle category (Vietcetera, from a search summary: S10). Decree 168/2024/ND-CP (in force 1 January 2025) raised fines. This is **conditional on licence country**. Many sources are tourism blogs, not government texts; verify with the Vietnamese embassy.
- **Unlicensed travellers**: two of the four hold no licence of any kind and must not ride. Pillion on an easy-rider bike is lawful for the passenger, but a 19-year-old British tourist died on an easy-rider tour in April 2026 and Tuyên Quang tightened rules (S08, S09). We do not use it.
- **Car self-drive**: possible in theory (home car licence plus 1968 IDP plus local insurance), but rental firms apply minimum ages (often 21) and deposits (from about 5 million VND), no rental was confirmed for these remote roads, and the Dutch advice recommends a car with driver (S05). The two licensed drivers could share driving, but mountain roads, fog and landslides make a hired driver the safer default.
- **Plan**: private seven-seat car with driver, Day 3–11, booked via a licensed Hanoi operator. Written inclusions: fuel, driver food/lodging, parking, insurance, border permits.
- **Fallbacks**: limousine van Hanoi–Hà Giang 6.5–7 h (350,000–450,000 VND); Hanoi–Cao Bằng 5–7 h from about 340,000 VND (S12).
- **Night buses** exist; not used.
- **Border permit**: see section 7.

## 9. Alternatives considered

- **Hà Giang loop by easy rider or self-riding motorbike**: rejected (licences, fatal accident, tightened rules).
- **Sa Pa, Y Tý, Bát Xát, Mù Cang Chải**: Y Tý is about 2,000 m, rough roads, weak trekking infrastructure; terraces not at their best in March; Sa Pa is a popular mainstream region. Possible extension only with more days.
- **Hanoi–Ninh Bình–Hạ Long**: the classic circuit; deliberately excluded.
- **Camping**: limited; unverified; homestays chosen.
- **February variant**: after about 15 February, colder and foggier.
