# D09 — Egypt: Pyramids, an Oracle Oasis and the Theban Tombs

Research date: 2026-11-27. Agent A09. All prices are planning estimates unless marked otherwise.

## Overview

Thirteen days (13–25 March 2027): Cairo (Islamic Cairo, Saqqara, Dahshur, Giza), overnight bus to Siwa Oasis with three nights in town (no desert excursions or camp), overnight bus back, a flight to Luxor (Karnak, West Bank, Dendera, Abydos) and the Abela sleeper train home. Self-drive is not part of the plan.

**Redesign decision.** The brief's concept included Bahariya, the Black and White Deserts and a Bahariya–Siwa link. These were removed because (a) the UK FCDO advises against all but essential travel on the Bahariya (Al Bawiti)–Siwa road, which is closed with military checkpoints; (b) the Netherlands marks "western Egypt (except Siwa town)" orange; (c) a secondary search summary reports a Smartraveller statement of a ban on safaris and camping near Bahariya (unverified, page timed out). Siwa itself is a named exception in FCDO and Netherlands advice and in France's "oasis zone" wording, so it was kept, reached only by the Alexandria–Marsa Matruh road. The trip stays coherent (ancient civilisations, desert, Siwa), but the White Desert camping highlight is lost.

## February vs March

- **February:** day 19–25 C, night 6–10 C (Siwa nights can approach 0 C in rare cases per station summaries). Ramadan starts about 8 or 9 February: shorter site hours, daytime restaurant closures outside tourist areas, lively iftar. Fewer crowds in Siwa but a colder dune night. Rated fair.
- **March:** day 23–30 C, night 11–15 C. Eid al-Fitr about 9–11 March (3-day holiday, domestic crowds), so start on 13 March. Khamsin dust and heat peaks (33 C+ in Luxor) are possible. Rated good.
- Ramadan dates: Dar al-Ifta announces after moon sighting; sources give 8 or 9 February for the start and 9–11 March for Eid (Wego, Misr Connect, timeanddate).

## Itinerary rationale

Days 1–4 Cairo area (history), day 4 night bus, days 5–8 Siwa (three nights in an eco-lodge, day 7 gardens, lakes and craft workshops inside the town zone, day 8 flexible), day 9 flight to Luxor, days 9–12 Upper Egypt, day 12 sleeper train, day 13 flight home. Flexible days: 8 and 12. Culture days 3–4; outdoor days in Siwa and Luxor are about 4; remote nights: 3 overnight transport legs; no camping.

## Transport and driving legality

- **Self-drive:** not recommended. France advises avoiding night driving outside cities and warns of poorly signposted restricted desert zones; FCDO notes accident risk. Two travellers hold licences but are not needed.
- **Hired driver:** Cairo and Luxor day trips (approx 50–110 EUR per van-day estimate).
- **Bus Cairo–Siwa:** reported at about 480 EGP, 22:00–06:50 (one 2026 guide), aggregators 9h45–14h and $26–30. Unverified return schedules. Must follow the Matruh road, not the Bahariya road.
- **Sleeper train:** Seat61 gives Abela foreigner fares of $110–170 one-way; northbound train 87 departs Aswan 17:15, arrives Cairo 06:25; Luxor departure time not stated in the retrieved text.
- **Domestic flight Cairo–Luxor:** about 1 h 10 min, 55–120 EUR estimate.
- **Motorcycles:** none; unlicensed travellers ride only as passengers.

**Classification: conditional.** The redesigned route avoids all-travel and orange areas on its main legs, but: the governments disagree on the Western Desert; Siwa is about 50 km from the Libyan border with a stated infiltration and kidnapping risk; desert excursions depend on permit regimes that cannot be verified before booking; and one unresolved source (Smartraveller) reports a camping ban near Bahariya. It is not eligible for the ranking until these are resolved. A safer variant without Siwa (Cairo–Fayoum–Luxor–Aswan) would be eligible on current wording but loses the desert/oasis identity.
