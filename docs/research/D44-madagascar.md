# D44 — Madagascar: Down the RN7 Between Rain and Sandstone

Researched 2026-11-28. Origin: participant proposal. Sources S01–S16 are listed in `data/destinations/D44-madagascar.json`.

## Overview

A 14-day, driver-guided road trip down the RN7 from Antananarivo to Toliara: Antsirabe, Ambositra, Ranomafana rainforest, Fianarantsoa, Ambalavao/Anja, Isalo, Toliara and the Reniala spiny forest, then a domestic flight back to Antananarivo for the night flight home. It is designed around the one thing that dominates February and March in Madagascar: the wet and cyclone season.

## Screening verdict

- **Seasonality: poor to fair.** February is the peak of the wet season and often the highest cyclone-risk month; March is the last wet month with fewer cyclones (S16). FCDO notes that many secondary roads are impassable except by 4x4 from December to April (S02). Steppes Travel calls January to March cyclone season with places closed and roads possibly impassable (S15).
- **What is out of scope in this window:** the Tsingy de Bemaraha is generally closed in the wet season (open roughly April/May to November, Grand Tsingy from about June; S14), so the Avenue of the Baobabs/Morondava leg (which uses the same western roads, named by the Netherlands as an elevated-risk interior area, S07) is not recommended. The east coast and the north are the most cyclone-exposed. Andringitra/Pic Boby: no source I could fetch states its wet-season status, so it is not in the plan (unverified). Andasibe-Mantadia (indri) is reachable from Antananarivo in about 3-4 h and is the first thing to add if a day is freed; it is in the wet east, so it was left out of the core.
- **Realistic core:** RN7 plus the south-west drier zone (Isalo, Toliara and Reniala), with two highland towns and the Ranomafana forest in between.
- **Flights:** direct Air France from Paris; fare statistics show about 808 EUR average in March and 900 EUR in February, with Air France direct examples of 920-932 EUR (S13). Representative, not a live quote.
- **Local costs:** moderate, but a private driver-guide for 11 days and mandatory park guides make a four-person trip add up.
- **Security:** conditional (see below).
- **Independent-travel feasibility:** self-drive is rare and not recommended. Driver-guide is standard.

## February vs March

February: heavy and continuous rain on the plateau and Ranomafana, higher cyclone risk, rated poor. March: still wet, rated fair, with more sunny days. The recommended window is late March, with April the true shoulder month (outside the brief). Easter 2027 is 28 March and Martyrs' Day 29 March (public holiday). Ramadan and Lunar New Year are not relevant.

## Itinerary rationale

Day 1 flight; 2 Antananarivo; 3 Antsirabe; 4 Ambositra; 5-6 Ranomafana; 7 Fianarantsoa and Ambalavao/Anja; 8 Isalo; 9-10 Isalo (camping night 10); 11 Toliara; 12 Reniala (flexible); 13 domestic flight and night flight; 14 arrival. Daily driving stays at or under about 5-6 h, and everything is daytime. Day 12 absorbs RN7 delays. If the flight Toliara-Antananarivo is cancelled the fallback is a long daytime drive, which is not recommended; the group should fly up the night before the international flight if the schedule allows.

## Transport and driving legality

A driver-guide with a 4x4 or minibus is standard. Forum reports put the price at 40-60 EUR per day plus fuel (S12, anecdotal; 50 EUR cited for 2024). Self-drive: two of the four hold licences; an IDP and home licence would normally be needed, but the roads (potholes, heavy trucks, no lighting; S02), night robbery risk and cyclone washouts make it unsuitable. Nobody rides motorbikes without a licence.

## Security assessment

Checked 2026-11-28.

- **UK FCDO (S01-S03):** advice current, last updated 16 Nov 2026. No region is under advice against travel. Protests in late September/early October 2025 involved violence and looting; a transitional government was formed on 17 Oct 2025. Africa Corps (Russian paramilitary) is visible in Antananarivo. Robbery and violent theft are common, including in daylight at isolated beaches near Toliara. South: dahalo (cattle-rustler) attacks, convoys attacked on the RN7 between Antananarivo and Toliara, Ilakaka near Isalo named, the Ihosy-Toliara-Fort Dauphin area tense; use a recognised tour operator and fly to Fort Dauphin. West: night highway robberies; high vigilance on the RN7.
- **France Diplomatie (S06):** updated 13 Nov 2026; public order calm for several months; vigilance around the 25 September anniversary; Ebola alert (13 Nov 2026) barring foreign nationals from or via DR Congo and requiring undertakings for Kenya/Nigeria; El Niño alert (5 Nov 2026), measles. The zone map page could not be retrieved, so no colour is claimed.
- **Netherlands (S07):** yellow overall; higher risk of armed robbery in the central-west interior toward Bemaraha and in Anosy/Betroka; regular demonstrations; storms November to April.
- **US State (S08):** page returned HTTP 403; search summaries say Level 3 in Sep-Oct 2025 then Level 2 from 5 Dec 2025. Unverified.
- **Smartraveller:** page timed out twice; not read.
- **Classification: conditional.** No advice against travel was found for the assessed route and it avoids the named high-risk areas (Bemaraha, Fort Dauphin, Anosy, Betroka, Nosy Be), but the RN7 south of Ihosy and the Isalo/Ilakaka area are named by the FCDO, the political situation is a transitional government, France's map and the US level could not be verified, and cyclone and flood risk is highest in the window.

## Health

CDC (S09): malaria outside Antananarivo (prophylaxis recommended), hepatitis A, typhoid, rabies, schistosomiasis in fresh water, plague listed (no seasonal detail in the page read; plague is commonly reported to peak in the austral spring and summer, which should be verified with health authorities). FCDO notes mpox. Ebola entry measures were in place in November 2026.

## Visa

FCDO (S04): up to 15 days with no visa and a 10 EUR fee; otherwise 30- or 60-day tourist visa on arrival, price not stated. Budget uses 35 EUR realistic; unverified.

## Signature experiences

Isalo canyons with a camping night; Ranomafana rainforest and night walk; Anja ring-tailed lemurs and Ambalavao paper; Reniala spiny forest and Vezo coast.

## Alternatives considered

- Andasibe-Mantadia (indri): short from Antananarivo, wet east, optional swap for day 12.
- Tsingy and baobabs: excluded (closed in wet season; Netherlands interior risk).
- Andringitra/Pic Boby: wet-season status not verified.
- Dry-season trip (May to October) would be the proper way to see Madagascar; this proposal is not recommended unless the dates are fixed.
- A cheaper variant is not credible.

## Open questions and reassessment triggers

1. Cyclone season outlook and El Niño alert; any named storm in the Mozambique Channel or Indian Ocean.
2. Live France map, Smartraveller and US levels.
3. Andringitra status; park fee tariff; Madagascar Airlines reliability and schedule.
