# D45 — Kazakhstan: Cave Mosques, Chalk Canyons and the Steppe Road to Nauryz

Researched 2026-11-28. Origin: participant proposal (2026-11-28). Kazakhstan-only; deliberately different from D21 (Central Asia).

## Overview
14 days, assumed 9–22 March 2027 so that day 13 is Nauryz (21 March). Paris CDG → Astana (open-jaw), Astana (day 2), flight to Aktau, four days (4–7) by driven 4x4 with three tent nights in Mangystau, flight to Shymkent (day 8), Otrar, Arystan Bab, Turkistan and Sauran (days 9–11), overnight train to Almaty, Almaty with a Tamgaly day and Nauryz (12–13), flight home (day 14).
Difference from D21: D21's Kazakh part is Almaty, Medeu/Shymbulak and Charyn. D45 uses Almaty only as arrival-by-train, Nauryz and departure city; its core (Mangystau, Turkistan Region, Astana, Tamgaly) is entirely different.

## Screening verdict
- Seasonality: March good, February fair (Astana nights about -20 C; Mangystau tent nights well below freezing).
- Flights: no non-stop Paris–Astana found; one stop assumed. Representative price EUR 750 pp (open-jaw, estimate).
- Local costs: low relative to Europe, but the Mangystau 4x4 (EUR 400/day/car, 2027 season) dominates.
- Security: eligible (see below). Cultural interest: high (Sufi pilgrimage, UNESCO, Silk Road). Adventure: moderate, guided. Independent travel: limited; driven transport only.

## February vs March
Feb: Astana days -12 to -5 C, Mangystau about 0 to 5 C by day with wind, no Nauryz. March: Mangystau days 8–11 C, nights 0–3 C (Wanderlog, aggregate), south 10–15 C, Almaty 5–12 C; Nauryz 21–23 March. Ramadan ends about 9 March; Kazakhstan is secular, minimal impact. Lunar New Year: not relevant.

## Itinerary rationale
Mangystau is front-loaded because it is the weather-sensitive part, with flexible day 7 absorbing delays. The south follows because it is mild; the train experience replaces an internal flight; Almaty is saved for Nauryz and the departure. Baikonur is excluded: it is Russian-leased, needs a permit about ten days ahead and tours are tied to launch dates (S17). Aksu-Zhabagly reserve is dropped: guide and permit are mandatory and its season is April–September in most sources. Astana's architecture is kept to one day because of the cold.

## Transport and driving legality
Self-drive not recommended (winter tracks, scarce fuel; UK FCDO notes 1968 IDP is needed). Mangystau by operator 4x4 with driver (max 4 people). Hired car with driver elsewhere. Domestic flights: SCAT, Air Astana and FlyArystan serve Astana–Aktau (21–24 a week). Aktau–Shymkent not verified: fall back via Astana or Almaty. No motorcycles.

## Signature experiences
Four-day Mangystau camp; Beket-Ata; Yasawi mausoleum and Otrar/Sauran; sleeper train and Nauryz.

## Alternatives considered
Cheaper variant: early-booking 4x4 rate and guesthouses (about EUR 1,610). Self-drive Mangystau rejected. Almaty mountains in winter rejected for overlap with D21.
