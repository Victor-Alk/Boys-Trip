# D33 — Spain: From the Gaitanes Gorge to the Almería Desert

Researched 2026-11-28. Participant proposal. Only the Caminito prices, AEMET Granada normals and the three advisories were read from primary pages; everything else is flagged as an estimate.

## Overview
12-day self-drive loop in inland Andalusia (about 1,230 km): Málaga, Caminito del Rey, Ronda, Setenil, Córdoba, Granada, Capileira (Alpujarras), Trevélez, Tabernas desert, Cabo de Gata (2 camping nights), Málaga. Window 3–14 March 2027 (Wed–Sun).

## Screening verdict
- Seasonality: March good to excellent; February good (colder, deeper snow).
- Flights: CDG–AGP nonstop about 2 h 40 min; fare is a planning estimate (€190).
- Local costs: mid-range; realistic total about €1,619 pp (within preferred budget).
- Security: eligible.
- Cultural interest: high (Mezquita, Alhambra, Alpujarras). Adventure: moderate (gorge walkway, snow day).
- Independent travel: very easy; least remote of the portfolio.

## February vs March
Granada airport normals (AEMET, 1981–2010): Feb 15.4/1.6 °C, 38 mm; Mar 19.0/3.8 °C, 32 mm. Capileira (about 1,400 m) is colder (estimate). March preferred; February is cheaper and snowier. Semana Santa 2027 starts 21 March (Palm Sunday), Easter 28 March; Fallas Valencia about 15–19 March (not on route); Cádiz Carnival about 4–14 February (not on route, one source). Ramadan not relevant.

## Itinerary rationale
Gorge first (Caminito needs a booked slot, Tue–Sun), cities in the middle (Alhambra slot fixed), mountains next, desert coast last so the two camping nights fall in the mildest climate. Contingency days: 8 (snow day fallback) and 11. Day 3 (about 220 km) and Day 12 (about 285 km) are the longest drives. Seville and the Canary Islands were not used.

## Transport and driving legality
EU/EEA licences are valid in Spain; other licences depend on origin (DGT). Two named drivers on the rental. No motorcycles. Free autovías; Granada and Córdoba low-emission-zone rules not checked. Madrid–Andalusia high-speed rail suspended after the 28 October 2026 Toledo collision (France Diplomatie, FCDO); the car route is not affected.

## Camping
Free camping for leisure is prohibited in Andalusia (Decreto 26/2018 context, secondary summaries only); the natural park may be stricter. Use licensed campsites; bungalow fallback.

## Security assessment (checked 2026-11-28)
- France Diplomatie: no colour-coded warning in the retrieved text; rail disruption (28 Oct 2026) and autumn flooding notice. Security map not readable from the page text.
- UK FCDO: no warning against travel shown; strike and rail notices (updated 10 Nov 2026).
- Netherlands Worldwide: green, updated 17 Nov 2026; terrorism possible.
Ordinary risks: theft, mountain weather, flash floods in ramblas. Status: eligible.

## Signature experiences
Caminito del Rey; Alhambra and Albaicín; Capileira snow day with a certified guide; Tabernas and Cabo de Gata.

## Alternatives considered
Canary Islands (Teide, La Gomera): milder and more volcanic, but longer flights and less cultural. Pyrenees/Aragon: snow-bound in winter. Seville added would raise city share; skipped. Cheaper variant: hostales, no guide, hostels = about €1,065.
