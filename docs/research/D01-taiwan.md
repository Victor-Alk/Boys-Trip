# D01 — Taiwan: Between Mountains and the Pacific

Research date: 2026-11-27. Agent A01. All local prices are planning estimates unless stated.

## Overview

A 13-day focused regional expedition (not a full circumnavigation): Taipei, Hualien and Taroko, the East Rift Valley, a camping stretch on the Pacific side near Taitung, Sandimen (Paiwan/Rukai), Tainan, Alishan, Taipei. Rental 7-seater Days 3–8 only; rail, HSR and bus elsewhere.

**Why regional rather than a circumnavigation.** A full loop (about 1,000+ km) needs either many long driving days or heavy reliance on rail, and its western leg (Taichung–Hsinchu–Taoyuan) is dense urban sprawl that adds little. The focused route keeps the east coast and the south-west, which carry the identity (mountains, Pacific, indigenous cultures, Tainan), and leaves 2 flexible days.

## Screening verdict

- Seasonality: March good, February fair (northeast monsoon, Lunar New Year).
- Flights: representative EVA Air fares about EUR 811–924 return; planning figure EUR 880.
- Local cost: about EUR 1,274 + 12 % contingency per person.
- Security: eligible (see below); France map colour unverified.
- Cultural interest: high (Tainan, indigenous communities, temples).
- Adventure: moderate; Taroko trails closed; high-mountain permits excluded.
- Independent travel: very feasible; licence acceptance must be checked.

## February vs March

February: day 17–23 °C, night 11–16 °C on the lowlands, frequent drizzle on the Hualien coast and in the north, Alishan cold (about 3–7 °C at night), occasional snow on Hehuanshan with overnight road closures. Lunar New Year 6 Feb 2027, government break 4–10 Feb, and Peace Memorial Day weekend 27 Feb – 1 Mar mean crowded trains and higher prices. March: 19–25 °C, still showery in the east, fewer holiday crowds, cherry blossom starts at Alishan. Preferred window 10–22 March 2027. Ramadan is not relevant in Taiwan.

Caveat: climate figures are from commercial sites (weather2travel, weather-and-climate) which disagree on Hualien rainfall (about 140 vs 290 mm for March); CWA normals were not retrievable.

## Itinerary rationale

See the JSON for day-by-day. Longest driving day: Day 7 Taitung to Sandimen (about 4 h, 180 km). Flexible days: 5 (camping can swap to a guesthouse), 9 (Tainan), 12 (Taipei buffer before the evening flight). Camping on Days 5–6 at licensed campsites.

### Current closures (checked 27 Nov 2026)
- Taroko (April 2024 earthquake and later typhoons): a March 2026 guide reports Highway 8 open only in time windows to self-drive vehicles; an August 2026 guide lists windows 06:30–08:00, 10:00–10:05, 12:00–13:00, 15:00–15:05 and 17:00–17:30 (unconfirmed against the official page, which could not be read). Shakadang, Zhuilu, Swallow Grotto and Baiyang trails reported closed; Tianxiang open. Park notices closed ecological protection area trails for Typhoon Champi (21 Oct 2026). The plan therefore uses only the road and open Tianxiang walks.
- Highway 9: lane closure near Duoliang (km 401) after Typhoon Champi, October 2026; status unknown. Day 7 relies on it; fallback is rail plus a taxi or an added day.
- Alishan Forest Railway suspended 23–24 Oct 2026 for Typhoon Champi; status after that not confirmed.
- Southern Cross-Island Highway (Highway 20) and Central Cross-Island Highway western sections were not used; their 2026 status could not be verified, so they are excluded from the route.

## Transport & driving legality

- Cars: rental firms generally require home licence, passport, credit card and an IDP (1949 Geneva or 1968 Vienna) or a translated licence; non-governmental IDPs are refused. Acceptance depends on licence country; the DGH reciprocal-country table was not retrievable, so no country is confirmed. Validity period for visitors is described inconsistently by sources (not resolved).
- Second driver: NT$180 per day at Hotai's Tainan HSR branch; must be present at pick-up.
- Scooters: nobody without the legal licence rides; IDP with motorcycle endorsement is typically required. Alternatives: car, bus, rail, bicycle in Chishang.
- Rail: Taroko Express to Hualien, Puyuma to Taitung, HSR Chiayi–Taipei. Lunar New Year tickets sell out within hours (700,000 HSR tickets in an hour in 2026).

## Signature experiences

Taroko in time windows with a community-run Truku tour; Pacific and valley camping with Chishang cycling; a night in Sandimen with craft workshops; Tainan across four centuries; Alishan sunrise and forest railway.

## Alternatives considered

- Full circumnavigation: rejected (above).
- Rail-and-bus-only variant: saves about EUR 250–300 per person (no rental, no camping gear), loses Highway 11, Chishang cycling and Sandimen. Would bring the total to about EUR 2,000.
- Central Cross-Island and Yushan/Xueshan high mountains: excluded; permits and snow-season certification requirements and unverified road status.
- Kenting/south tip: not included; time.
