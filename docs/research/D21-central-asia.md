# D21 — Central Asia: Tiled Cities and Desert Fortresses of the Silk Road

Added at the group's request (participant proposal, 2026-11-28). Research date 2026-11-28.

## Overview
Uzbekistan (Tashkent, Samarkand, Bukhara, Khiva, Khorezm fortresses), a day trip to Penjikent and Sarazm in Tajikistan, and south-east Kazakhstan (Almaty, Medeu, Charyn Canyon). 14 days, 13–26 March 2027, mostly by Afrosiyob train, hired driver and two flights. One yurt night.

## Screening verdict
- **Seasonality:** March good, February fair. Samarkand March mean 15.2/4.6 C (73 mm, 3 snowy days); Almaty March 9.9/-0.2 C (8 snowy days). February is colder (Almaty 2.7/-6.2 C, 13 snowy days) and entirely inside Ramadan.
- **Tajikistan as a full country:** Dushanbe, the Fann Mountains, Iskanderkul and the Pamir Highway are snowbound/winter-closed in Feb-Mar and are not planned. This is why Tajikistan is only a Penjikent/Sarazm day (flexible day 5, fallback Shahrisabz). Combining three countries is coherent only in this reduced form; the Uzbekistan-Kazakhstan two-country version is the core.
- **Kazakhstan:** Charyn Canyon and Almaty/Medeu are feasible in March. Big Almaty Lake and Kolsai are winter-limited, and Kolsai is in a border zone (permit), so excluded.
- **Security:** eligible on the assessed route (see below). **Budget:** realistic about EUR 1,993 pp, borderline "within preferred".
- **Independence:** high on rail and with hired drivers; self-drive not recommended.

## February vs March
February: cheap and quiet, but Ramadan, snow, freezing nights and bad air in Tashkent/Almaty. March (13-26): Ramadan over (Eid al-Fitr about 9-10 March), Navruz on 21 March (day 9, Khiva), warmer, wetter. Navruz means higher prices and full trains.

## Itinerary rationale
Rail corridor first (Tashkent-Samarkand-Bukhara), then the one hard day (Bukhara-Khiva, 450 km), a desert fortress and yurt day, a two-flight transfer day, and Kazakhstan for contrast. Flexible days: 5 and 13.

## Transport and driving legality
Afrosiyob trains between Tashkent, Samarkand and Bukhara; hired drivers elsewhere; no rental. Two licence holders not needed. No motorcycles. IDP rules not verified in detail since self-drive is not planned.

## Security assessment (checked 2026-11-28)
- France Diplomatie: Uzbekistan (updated 15 Sep 2026): Afghan border zone/Termez formally advised against; rest heightened vigilance. Tajikistan (15 Sep 2026): Afghan border formally advised against, Uzbek border off-road (mines) advised against, rest yellow, GBAO by special permit. Kazakhstan (19 Oct 2026): only the Semipalatinsk nuclear-test zone formally advised against; permits for restricted border strips.
- UK FCDO: no against-travel areas read; cautions on Afghan and Kyrgyz borders; Tajikistan updated 19 Aug 2026 (armed clashes near the Afghan border, 13 Aug 2026 incident in Darvoz, terrorism likely to be attempted, Nowruz caution).
- Netherlands: Tajikistan, Kazakhstan yellow; Uzbekistan yellow with Termez and 5 km border strip orange.
- US: Uzbekistan 1, Kazakhstan 1, Tajikistan 2 (reconsider Afghan border and GBAO).
- Classification: **eligible** for the route that avoids those areas. Honest caveat: Tajik terrorism warnings and mine hazards are material; the Penjikent day is optional and flexible and the status should be revisited if advice changes. A stricter reading could rate it conditional because of the Tajik leg; the Uzbekistan-Kazakhstan core has no such issue.

## Signature experiences
Samarkand at dawn; Navruz in Khiva; Khorezm fortresses and heated yurt night; Charyn Canyon.

## Alternatives considered
Uzbekistan only (cheaper by about EUR 300, drops the Tajik visa and the flight to Almaty); Uzbekistan + Kazakhstan without Penjikent. Dushanbe, Fann and the Pamir rejected for season and security.
