# D02 — Iraq: research notes (checked 2026-11-27)

## Overview
Concept: Mesopotamia and its ancient civilisations, religious heritage and contemporary Iraqi culture. Imagined route: Baghdad (Al-Mutanabbi Street, Kadhimiya, Samarra) – Babylon – Najaf / Karbala – Uruk and the Ziggurat of Ur – Chibayish in the Ahwar marshes – a Kurdistan Region extension (Erbil Citadel, Rawanduz, Amedi). Nippur and the Mandaean and Christian heritage were considered; no separate route could be built that avoids advised-against regions.

## Screening verdict
**Security: EXCLUDED.** Seasonality fair-to-good (March best); flights indicative about EUR 600; local costs dominated by mandatory-style guides/drivers; cultural interest exceptional; adventure modest; independent travel not feasible.

## February vs March
February: Ramadan begins about 8 Feb (limited daytime dining), cold and wet in Kurdistan. March: Eid al-Fitr about 9-10 Mar, then mild days (about 21-26 C in the south), Nowruz 20-21 Mar in Kurdistan. March preferred (window 11-23 March, indicative only).

## Itinerary rationale
Informational only. The four regions (Baghdad/Abbasid, Babylon + pilgrim cities, Sumer + marshes, Kurdistan) are listed as a geographic overview with no legs or days.

## Transport and driving legality
Self-drive not realistic. Hired driver and guide only; domestic flights where needed. Two of four travellers hold car licences; nobody rides motorcycles. IDP rules not verified.

## Budget workings (indicative, EUR per person, flights incl.)
Realistic local: baggage 60; domestic flight 90; local taxis 5; hired driver 11 days x 130 / 4 = 357.5; hotels 24 room-nights x 50 / 4 = 300; food 13 x 25 = 325; water 13 x 3 = 39; entrances 10 x 5 = 50; activities 3 x 60 / 4 = 45; guides 8 x 100 / 4 = 200; insurance 150. Visa and permits are unknown (null, excluded from totals) so the true figure is higher. All figures are planning estimates, not quotes.
