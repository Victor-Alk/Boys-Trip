# D13 — Philippines: Island Chains Between El Nido and Coron

Research date: 2026-11-27. Agent A13. Security status: **conditional**.

## Overview
A 14-day route through northern Palawan: fly Manila to Busuanga, ferry to El Nido, a four-night community-run boat expedition (Daracouton Bay, Linapacan, south Culion, west Busuanga) to Coron, then Tagbanua-managed Coron Island sites, Calauit and Culion (former leprosy colony, museum). It avoids the standard day-tour circuit (El Nido tours A–D, Coron joiner tours) for the core of the trip. Southern Palawan (Puerto Princesa, Sabang, Port Barton, San Vicente, Balabac, Bataraza) is deliberately not on the route.

- Flights: Paris–Manila about €640–1,100 (snapshots, not live). Realistic €820.
- Local costs: moderate except the expedition (about €650).
- Security: conditional (see below).
- Cultural interest: moderate-good (Culion, Tagbanua stewardship, crews).
- Adventure: moderate; boats, snorkelling, basic huts; no technical skills.
- Independent travel: workable by boats/vans/tricycles; no self-drive needed.

## February vs March

March: slightly drier, lighter winds on average, hotter; Holy Week begins about 21 March (Easter 28 March 2027), so window ends 17 March. Preferred window 4–17 March 2027. Ramadan: not relevant. No 2027 forecast is implied.

## Itinerary rationale
Days: 1 depart; 2 Manila→Busuanga/Coron; 3 ferry to El Nido; 4 buffer; 5–9 expedition (4 nights); 10 Coron Island boat day; 11 Calauit (flexible); 12 Culion (museum is weekdays only, day 12 is Monday 15 March); 13 return to Coron, fly Manila (flexible); 14 fly home. Direction El Nido→Coron follows Tao's standard route and the source advice that the sea is kinder in that direction. Tao suggests two spare days around an expedition; days 4 and 11 serve this.

## Security assessment (checked 2026-11-27)
- France Diplomatie (security page, updated 20 Nov 2026; vigilance map 11 Mar 2026): formally discouraged: western, central-western and south-western Mindanao and the maritime zone of the southern half of Palawan up to the Sulu archipelago; orange for parts of Mindanao. Abu Sayyaf kidnapping on land and at sea. Palawan trek/cruise coverage limited.
- UK FCDO (updated 15 July 2026): against all travel to western/central Mindanao and Sulu archipelago; against all but essential travel to remainder of Mindanao; Palawan, Balabac and Sulu Sea not mentioned.
- US State Department: direct fetch blocked (HTTP 403). A search summary reports Level 2 overall, Level 4 for the Sulu Archipelago and Sulu Sea, dated 1 Sept 2026. Unverified directly.
- Smartraveller: fetch timed out; a search summary (May 2026) reports reconsider eastern Mindanao and do not travel to other parts of Mindanao.
- Netherlands: the pages tried did not return the advice text; not assessed.
Classification: conditional. The route is north of about 11°N and avoids all listed areas, but the boundary of "southern half of Palawan" (France) and "Sulu Sea" (US) cannot be matched to Linapacan and the Culion coast from the text read. Reassess by reading the full pages and map.
