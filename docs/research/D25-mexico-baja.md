# D25 — Mexico — Baja: Whale Lagoons and Painted Canyons

Researched 2026-11-28. Participant proposal (proposedAt 2026-11-28). Distinct from D19 (Oaxaca and Chiapas).

## Overview
Self-drive along Federal Highway 1 in **Baja California Sur only**: Loreto (Bahía de Loreto park), a beach camp at Bahía Concepción, San Ignacio as a base for the Ojo de Liebre and San Ignacio whale lagoons and the UNESCO rock paintings of the Sierra de San Francisco, then La Paz (Isla Espíritu Santo sea lions), Todos Santos and Cabo Pulmo. Open-jaw: Paris to Loreto, Los Cabos to Paris, 14 days, 3 camping nights.

## Screening verdict
- Seasonality: excellent in both months; whales in the lagoons about 15 Dec–30 Apr, peak mother-and-calf mid-Feb to mid-Mar (S07, S10).
- Flights: estimate only, about EUR 950 open-jaw via Mexico City; no live quote.
- Local costs: about EUR 1,612 per person before contingency (realistic).
- Security: eligible, as the route stays out of Baja California state (see below).
- Cultural interest: strong rock art and Jesuit mission towns, though much of the trip is wildlife.
- Adventure: guided mule/foot trek, sea kayaking, snorkelling, beach camping.
- Independent feasibility: good with daylight-only driving and pre-booked guides and boats.

## February vs March
February: peak calves, quiet roads, cold desert nights (about 7-10 C), cooler sea (about 20 C). March: warmer, slightly higher demand, whales begin to leave in April; Semana Santa 21–28 March 2027 must be avoided. Recommended window 20 Feb–5 Mar 2027. Climate figures for La Paz come from S11; Guerrero Negro and the sierra are estimates.

## Itinerary rationale
North first, so that the long cross-peninsula runs (San Ignacio to Loreto 270 km, Loreto to La Paz 355 km) are done mid-trip and the finale is a short reef and sea-lion stretch. Day 6 (lagoon) and day 11 (Espíritu Santo) are weather buffers; day 9 is also flexible. The longest driving days are 10 (4.5 h) and 12 (about 5 h).

## Transport and driving legality
One high-clearance SUV or crew-cab pickup from Loreto airport to Los Cabos airport; both ends are in Baja California Sur, so no inter-state restriction is expected, but the one-way fee was not found. Mexican third-party liability insurance is mandatory and normally bought at the counter; credit-card cover does not replace it. Two licensed drivers, both listed on the contract. Daylight driving only (livestock, trucks, potholes). Expect routine military and National Guard checkpoints on Mex-1. No motorcycles. Fuel: Pemex stations in every major town, fill when below half.

## Security assessment (checked 2026-11-28)
- France Diplomatie, Sécurité page (updated 15 Sep 2026; zone list updated 11 Mar 2026): Baja California Nord is orange; Tijuana and Tecate red; Baja California Sur falls in "reste du pays en vigilance renforcée" (yellow). An El Niño weather alert dated 4 Nov 2026 is listed (text not retrieved) (S01, S05).
- UK FCDO (updated 24 Nov 2026): all-but-essential for Tijuana and Tecate; Baja California Sur unaffected (S02, S06).
- Nederland Wereldwijd (changed 26 Nov 2026): Baja California yellow; border cities orange; BCS not listed. Warns against travel after dark and fake police stops of rental cars (S03).
- US State Department: page returned HTTP 403; secondary reports say Level 2 (BCS) and Level 3 (Baja California); **unverified** (S04).
- Classification: **eligible** for a route kept within BCS. Originally proposed concepts (Tijuana/Ensenada entry, Valle de los Cirios, Bahía de los Ángeles) would cross an orange state under French advice and were removed. Reassess if any authority raises BCS, or if the US page shows Level 3+.

## Signature experiences
1. Grey whales from a licensed panga at Ojo de Liebre and San Ignacio (about EUR 160 pp).
2. Guided mule or foot trek to Great Mural sites with an INAH custodian and local guide (about EUR 75 pp).
3. Espíritu Santo and Los Islotes sea-lion snorkel (about EUR 100 pp; closed 1 Jun–31 Aug).
4. Beach camping at Bahía Concepción.

## Alternatives considered
Fly into Tijuana or San Diego and drive Mex-1 south: rejected, as it enters orange Baja California state and requires passing near Tijuana (red in French advice). Valle de los Cirios and Bahía de los Ángeles: outside the route. A cheaper variant: Los Cabos in and out, with La Paz, Todos Santos and Cabo Pulmo only and no lagoons, saving drive time but losing the core whale experience.
