# D16 — Senegal: Mangrove Islands, Dunes and the River Mouth

Verification date: 2026-11-27. Agent A16. Planning notes, not quotations.

## Overview
A 13-day route (11 days on the ground) designed to go beyond the classic Dakar circuit: Petite Côte fishing towns, the shell island of Fadiouth, two nights by pirogue in the Saloum Delta (UNESCO-listed), Kaolack, a tented dune camp at Lompoul, Saint-Louis and the Djoudj bird sanctuary in peak season, then Dakar for the last two days. A hired vehicle with driver handles roads; pirogues handle the delta.

## Screening verdict
- Seasonality: excellent in February (dry, 27–33 °C days, peak birds); good in March (hotter, fewer birds).
- Flights: Paris–Dakar direct (about 5.5 h); fares are an unverified estimate (€560 realistic).
- Local costs: low to moderate; about €1,127 local pp realistic before contingency.
- Security: eligible on the assessed route, with one borderline point (Djoudj near Mauritania).
- Cultural interest: Serer, Wolof, Lebu cultures, Sufi heritage, colonial history, music, food.
- Adventure: easy; pirogue, dunes, birding. No specialist skills.
- Independent travel: possible, but hired driver recommended.

## February vs March
- February: Saint-Louis normals 32.4/17.1 °C, 0.6 mm rain; harmattan haze; Djoudj best December–March; Ramadan starts about 8 Feb.
- March: 33.0/17.8 °C, 0.2 mm; hotter inland; Ramadan ends about 9 Mar, Korité about 9–10 Mar; a trip from about 11 March avoids Ramadan. Normals from the Saint-Louis climate table (S10); Dakar and inland ranges are approximations.
- Recommendation: 1–13 February 2027 (about 6 days inside Ramadan). Fallback: 11–23 March.

## Itinerary rationale
See the JSON days. One long drive only (Kaolack to Lompoul, about 290 km, 5 h). Two flexible days (Saloum day 5, Saint-Louis day 10, Dakar day 12). Touba is passed by, not visited; the Grand Magal is outside the window.

## Transport and driving legality
Self-drive is feasible on paved roads but not recommended (frequent accidents, unlit vehicles, police checks; S01, S03). IDP requirement depends on licence country; not verified. Two of four hold car licences; additional-driver terms not checked. No motorcycles; passengers, hired driver, taxis, horse carts only. Sept-places taxis exist but suit four adults with luggage poorly. Dakar–Ziguinchor ferry and Transair flights are not used (Casamance off-route); schedules not verified.

## Security assessment (checked 2026-11-27)
- France Diplomatie (S01; updated 25 Oct and 25 Nov 2026): orange (déconseillé sauf raison impérative) = Mali border strip, part of Matam department near Mauritania, Casamance borders with Gambia and Guinea-Bissau away from main roads; yellow = Casamance; terrorist threat at the Mali border, particular caution in the east. No coloured zone on the assessed route.
- FCDO (S02, S03; updated 15 Oct 2026): no against-all/all-but-essential regions; particular care near the Mali and Mauritania borders; Casamance alert for clashes; frequent demonstrations.
- Nederland Wereldwijd (S06; updated 1 Sep 2026): orange on Casamance borders and eastern/north-eastern border areas with Mali and Mauritania; yellow elsewhere.
- US State Department (S12): travel.state.gov returned HTTP 403; Level 1 with Level 2 Casamance comes from a search summary of an OSAC page, unverified.
- Smartraveller: fetch timed out; not assessed.
- Political situation: only the France and FCDO pages were read (demonstrations, JOJ Dakar 2026 ended 13 Nov, March 2026 law on same-sex acts). The search budget ran out, so no broader political news review was done.
- Classification: eligible. Avoided: Casamance, Kédougou/Bassari country (Mali and Guinea borders; diphtheria outbreak mainly Kédougou), Podor and the Fouta (Mauritania border; Dutch orange), Touba. Borderline: Djoudj lies in the north near Mauritania. France and FCDO show no coloured zone there; the Dutch orange wording on north-eastern Mauritania border areas is ambiguous, so it is a reassessment trigger and A21 should verify the Dutch map.

## Signature experiences
Saloum Delta by pirogue; Fadiouth; Lompoul; Djoudj; Saint-Louis. Costs and organisation in the JSON.

## Alternatives considered
- Bassari country (Bandafassi, Dindéfélo): excluded for proximity to the Mali and Guinea borders and the Kédougou outbreak.
- Casamance (Cap Skirring, Ziguinchor): France permits the coastal zone, but the yellow/orange mix and Dutch orange borders led to skipping it.
- Podor and the Fouta by Bou el Mogdad: weekly Saturday sailings late October to early May at 680,000 FCFA (S07); Mauritania border and price; not recommended.
- Cheaper variant: shared taxis and guesthouses only, about €1,280.
- Wrestling (laamb) and mbalax: depend on fixtures; not verified.
