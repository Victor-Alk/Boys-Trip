# D03 — China: The Ancient Roads of Yunnan (research notes, checked 2026-11-27)

## Overview
14-day route (12–25 March 2027): Kunming – Jianshui – Yuanyang (Hani terraces) – Kunming – Dali – Shaxi – Lijiang – Tiger Leaping Gorge – Shangri-La – Kunming. High-speed rail for trunk legs, hired driver with minivan for mountain roads. No self-drive, no camping.

## Screening verdict
- Seasonality: March excellent, February fair (Lunar New Year holiday).
- Flights: no direct Paris–Kunming; one stop; estimate EUR 750 realistic (estimate, not a quote).
- Local costs: moderate; realistic local EUR 1,132 pp + 12% contingency.
- Security: eligible (see below), with France colour unverified.
- Cultural interest: very high (Hani, Bai, Naxi, Tibetan; Tea Horse Road; UNESCO sites).
- Adventure: moderate (gorge high trail, altitude to 3,300 m).
- Independent-travel feasibility: reduced. Foreign licences invalid; no easy self-drive; internet/payment friction.

## February vs March
- Spring Festival: New Year Sat 6 Feb 2027; official holiday 5–13 Feb (make-up days 31 Jan, 14 Feb) per State Council notice of 5 Nov 2026 [S10]. Chunyun (about 40 days, announced in January; 2026 ran 2 Feb–13 Mar and forecast 9.5 bn trips) means sold-out rail, higher fares and closed small businesses. No 2027 forecast exists yet.
- Climate (China Highlights averages): Lijiang Feb 7–19 C, March 10–23 C; Kunming Feb 5–17, March 8–21; rainy days 1–3 per month [S12, S13]. Shangri-La much colder (qualitative estimate).
- Yuanyang: terraces flooded late Nov–April; Dec–Jan peak fog, late Feb–March blossom; fog unreliable; one traveller post advises against March (reason unseen) [S18].
- Verdict: March best (12–25 March). Qingming holiday 3–5 April is outside the window.

## Itinerary rationale
Hani terraces and Jianshui first (warm, low altitude), then rail to the Bai heartland, Shaxi, Lijiang, gorge, and finally the highest point (Shangri-La 3,300 m) so altitude is gradual. Flexible days: 8 and 13. Excluded: Yubeng/Meili, Deqin high roads (winter snow), Myanmar border towns (Ruili, Zhenkang), Tibet.

## Transport and driving legality
- Rail: Kunming–Dali about 2–2.5 h; Kunming–Lijiang about 3–4 h [S14]; Lijiang–Shangri-La line opened 26 Nov 2023, about 1 h 10–18 min [S15]. Check 12306/Trip.com timetables; Kunming–Jianshui time not verified.
- Driving: foreign licences and IDPs not valid; foreigners need a Chinese temporary permit and may only drive rental cars in small categories [S11]; FCDO says a Chinese licence and insurance are needed [S02]. Secondary sources mention mutual recognition for some nationalities (unverified). Recommendation: no self-drive; hired driver.
- Motorcycles/scooters: not allowed without valid licence; alternatives listed in JSON.

## Security assessment (checked 2026-11-27)
- UK FCDO: no against-travel wording for Yunnan; regional section lists Tibet, Xinjiang, China–Myanmar border (spillover) [S01, S04]; exit bans, detention up to 6 months in national-security cases, terrorism cannot be ruled out [S02].
- France Diplomatie: page updated 16 Nov 2026; alerts for Tibet floods, typhoons, El Nino, measles; vigilance colour is an image, could not be read [S05].
- Netherlands: yellow, no regional exception, updated 28 Aug 2026 [S06].
- Smartraveller: high degree of caution (listing 28 Sep 2026; page timed out) [S07].
- US State: Level 2 via search snippets; page returned 403 [S08].
- Belgian advice (snippet): shrapnel in Zhenkang from Myanmar fighting [S23].
- Classification: eligible, with border avoided. Re-verify France colour.

## Signature experiences
Hani terrace sunrise; Shaxi caravan inn night; Tiger Leaping Gorge high trail; Bai villages around Erhai.

## Alternatives considered
Shorter route without Yuanyang (saves a long driving day, loses best landscape). Domestic flight Kunming–Lijiang instead of rail. Self-drive rejected. Yubeng/Meili rejected for snow.

## Open questions / reassessment triggers
Visa-free beyond 31 Dec 2026 and per nationality; France colour; real fares; trail status; foreign-card limits (sources conflict: USD 5,000 per transaction/USD 50,000 per year vs CNY figures); hotel acceptance of foreigners; eSIM vs VPN reliability; Sanduo date.

Hotels register foreigners within 24 h [S03]; passport validity 6 months and 2 blank pages [S03]; Google/WhatsApp blocked, install VPN before arrival [S22]; Alipay/WeChat accept foreign cards, carry cash [S21]; wild camping not recommended (legality unclear, not verified).
