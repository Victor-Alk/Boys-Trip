# D43 — Oman: research notes

Added at the group's request (origin: participant-proposal, 2026-11-28). Checked 2026-11-28. Security status: **conditional**.

## Overview
Concept: "the Oman you haven't seen". A 12-day inland expedition for 13–24 March 2027 that leaves out the classic Muscat–Nizwa–Wahiba–Sur circuit. Route: Muscat (Seeb) → Wadi Bani Awf track and Balad Sayt → guided Snake Gorge descent → Al Hamra and Misfat Al Abriyyin → two tent nights on the Jebel Shams rim with the W6 Balcony Walk → Ibri, Bat, Al-Khutm and Al-Ayn (UNESCO tombs) → Jebel Akhdar plateau and Wadi Bani Habib → Nakhal and Rustaq → Muscat. About 1,300 km in one rented 4x4; day 10 is a contingency day.

## Screening verdict
- **Seasonality:** good in March after Eid: Saiq (Jebel Akhdar) mean 16.9/9.5 °C, Ibri 32.5/18.6 °C (S11, S12). Rims are cold at night (estimate 3–10 °C). Showers and wadi floods are possible.
- **Flights:** Oman Air has operated nonstop CDG–MCT (S26 is a 2021 schedule; current frequency not verified). Fare is an estimate of €560 (an Etihad cached listing showed from about €424–426 return, S25).
- **Local costs:** moderate; one 4x4 shared by four.
- **Security:** conditional (below). The most attractive "other Oman" regions (Dhofar, Duqm, Musandam, the Rub' al Khali from Thumrait) were dropped.
- **Cultural interest:** third-millennium BC necropolis, terrace villages, abandoned villages, forts.
- **Adventure:** guided canyon, exposed cliff path, steep 4x4 track; no technical climbing.
- **Independent travel:** feasible, with two licensed drivers; guides only for the canyon and the longer Jebel Akhdar walk.

## February vs March
February: cool, but Ramadan is expected from about 8 February (Canada says "around February 7", S06; most sources 8 February, S21), so only 1–7 February are free of it. March: Eid al-Fitr expected 9 or 10 March (moon-sighting dependent; no Omani official date). Leaving on Saturday 13 March starts after the holiday. Ramadan rules (S04, S05): eating, drinking and smoking in public in daylight are punishable, even for non-Muslims; many cafés close by day; guides fast. Lunar New Year (6 February) is not relevant. Easter is 28 March 2027, so the trip is before European spring holidays.

## Itinerary rationale
Day 2 is long after an overnight flight (190 km, ending on a track); if the flight arrives late, sleep in Seeb and use day 10 as slack. The canyon day (day 3) comes before the rim so that a washed-out canyon can be replaced by Little Snake Canyon or a rest day. Two rim nights allow the Balcony Walk to start early. Ibri is reached on day 6 so the tombs can be seen at late afternoon. Jebel Akhdar is climbed on a Friday (the 4x4 checkpoint). The terraces and Wadi Bani Habib have a full day. Day 9 descends via Nakhal and Rustaq; day 10 is the buffer; day 11 returns the car. Flight: Saturday departure and Wednesday return match the 2021 Oman Air pattern; not verified for 2027.

## Transport & driving legality
- **4x4 needed:** the Jebel Akhdar police checkpoint admits only 4x4 (S13, S14); Jebel Shams is paved then gravel near the rim (4x4 recommended, S14); Wadi Bani Awf is graded gravel with ledges (4x4 strongly recommended, S15).
- **Rental:** Fortuner 42 OMR per day (38 for 11+ days), super CDW +6 OMR, full cover +10 OMR, CDW excess 300 OMR, drivers 21–75 with one year of licence (one agency, S23); a Prado listing at USD 143 per day (listing posted 8 April 2026). Whether the contract allows the three mountain roads and off-road cover was not found: confirm in writing. Round trip from Muscat, so no one-way fee (Sixt allows domestic one-way under conditions, S22).
- **Licences:** FCDO says a UK photocard is valid for six weeks and that rental companies may require an IDP (S04). French-licence rules were not read. Two drivers on the contract; nobody rides motorcycles.
- **Empty Quarter:** France advises convoys of two vehicles minimum with desert experience (S01); the Netherlands advises a reliable guide (S05). Dropped because the access points in Dhofar are in a governorate where France advises extra care outside the coastal strip (S01) and a single-vehicle trip is not recommended.
- **Camping:** wild camping is popular but not on private land or in nature reserves (permits needed). Muscat Governorate needs a municipality licence, a 2022 report (S19), so no camping near Muscat. High ground, never in a wadi bed.

## Security assessment (checked 2026-11-28)
- **France Diplomatie (S01, last updated 17 July 2026; S02, 29 October 2026):** whole country in vigilance renforcée except the regions of Salalah, Duqm and Musandam, where travel is déconseillé sauf raison impérative, and the Yemen border zone (same). Outside the coastal strip in Dhofar extra care is needed. Flash floods (deadly in 2024) are dangerous for campers. Regional tensions may affect Oman.
- **UK FCDO (S04, updated 18 November 2026):** no area warning on the pages read; "risk of sudden escalation is high"; attacks possible across the region at short notice; flight cancellations and airspace closures to be expected. Terrorism cannot be ruled out (July 2024 shooting near a mosque in the Wadi Kabir area of Muscat).
- **Netherlands (S05, changed 8 September 2026):** orange for Musandam (Strait of Hormuz, regular missile and drone attacks) and the Yemen border; yellow for the rest. A search summary of an earlier version (24 July) named the ports of Duqm, Salalah and Sohar and industrial areas as orange; the current page does not. Sohar's port area is avoided anyway.
- **Germany (S07, unchanged since 29 October 2026):** warns against Musandam and the immediate Yemen border region; the situation is "äußerst volatil"; flight restrictions possible even for transit.
- **Canada (S06, 6 August 2026):** "avoid non-essential travel to Oman"; missile and drone attacks "have resumed and struck targets in Oman".
- **US State Department (S08) and Smartraveller (S09):** not fetched (HTTP 403, timeout). Search summaries say US Level 3 (Reconsider Travel; Yemen border Do Not Travel; updated 30 August 2026) and Australia "reconsider your need to travel". Re-read the live pages before booking.
- **Classification:** the redesigned route avoids all orange zones, so it is not excluded. It is **conditional** because the advice conflicts (Canada, US, Australia versus France, Netherlands, UK), because regional escalation can close airspace, and because insurers may not cover a trip against official advice. The route is inland and several hundred kilometres from Musandam. A July 2026 strike on Musandam reported in a search summary of the German page was not on the current text.

## Signature experiences
Snake Gorge descent; two nights on the Jebel Shams rim and the Balcony Walk; Bat, Al-Khutm and Al-Ayn at sunset; Jebel Akhdar terraces and Wadi Bani Habib with a guide; the Wadi Bani Awf crossing.

## Alternatives considered
- **Musandam (khors, dhow, Jebel Harim):** dropped: France orange, Netherlands orange (Strait of Hormuz attacks), Germany warns against it, and France says rental cars can no longer enter Musandam from the UAE.
- **Dhofar outside khareef (Salalah, Wadi Darbat, Wadi Dawkah, Mughsail, Shuwaymiyah):** dropped: Salalah region orange in France; the Yemen border zone is nearby.
- **Duqm, Al Wusta, Masirah, Bar Al Hikman:** dropped: Duqm region orange in France. Masirah and Bar Al Hikman were not separately listed, but the access runs through the Duqm/Al Wusta area; not recommended without a clearer reading.
- **Rub' al Khali from Thumrait/Shisr:** dropped (see above).
- **Cheaper variant:** economical scenario €1,157: own camping gear, simpler guesthouses and fewer paid activities.
