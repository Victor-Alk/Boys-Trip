# D27 — Turkey: Lycia and the Pisidian Highlands, Coast Path to Snowline

Participant proposal (28 November 2026). Research date 2026-11-28.

## Overview
A 13-day self-drive from Dalaman to Antalya (open-jaw): Lycian Way day stages (Ölüdeniz–Faralya, Kaş), Saklıkent Gorge, Xanthos–Letoon, Patara, Kekova by local boat, Myra, tent nights at Çıralı and the Chimaera, then inland to Termessos (about 1,000 m), Sagalassos (about 1,500 m) and Eğirdir. It goes beyond the classic Istanbul and Cappadocia circuit. Continent is set to "Europe" (the route is in the far south-west, a Mediterranean region next to the EU, not the Middle East of the south-east).

## Screening verdict
- Seasonality: March good, February fair (Antalya February mean 15.9/6.5 °C, 113 mm rain; March 18.4/8.3 °C, 95 mm; sea 17 °C; Eğirdir March 11.6/2.4 °C) [S09, S12].
- Flights: estimate only, EUR 260 realistic, open-jaw CDG–DLM / AYT–CDG, one stop.
- Local costs: planning figures; realistic about EUR 1,432 per person including flights and contingency (Affordable).
- Security: **conditional** (see below).
- Cultural interest, adventure, independent travel: high; waymarked paths, rentals, pensions.

## February vs March
Ramadan 2027 runs about 8 February to 9 March, Eid about 9–11 March. The window 13–25 March avoids both. February is wetter, Sagalassos and Eğirdir are often snowed, and inland daytime dining is limited during Ramadan.

## Itinerary rationale
Coast first (mild, early March snow melts), mountains later (day 9), flexible days 9 and 11 absorb snow. Tent nights at Patara and Çıralı only (camping pensions). Walking is split into 3–5 h stages. Rejected or dropped segments are listed under Alternatives.

## Transport and driving legality
One rental car, two registered drivers, one-way fee at Antalya. UK guidance requires a 1968 IDP and a green card [S05]; EU licences are normally accepted for short stays, to be confirmed with the renter. Nobody rides a motorcycle or scooter; taxis and dolmuş cover one-way walks.

## Security assessment
Sources checked 2026-11-28:
- France Diplomatie Sécurité (page dated 15 Sep 2026, zones updated 23 Mar 2026) [S01]: red = immediate Syria and Iraq borders, restrictions possible in Reyhanlı, Kırıkhan, Siirt, Şırnak, Hakkari; orange = Hatay, Kilis, Gaziantep, Şanlıurfa, Mardin, Diyarbakır, Batman; everything else yellow. Alerts [S02]: Iranian missile intercepted near Hatay (4 March 2026), regional escalation risk, Iran land border closed.
- UK FCDO (updated 18 Nov 2026) [S03, S04]: against all travel within 10 km of the Syria border; regional tensions "risk of sudden escalation is high".
- Netherlands (16 Oct 2026) [S06]: yellow, orange Şırnak/Hakkari/Habur, red borders.
- US State Department data feed (8 Jun 2026) [S07]: Level 2; do not travel to the Syria and Iraq border region.
Classification: the original ideas Şanlıurfa/Göbekli Tepe and Mardin are orange in France Diplomatie, so they were removed. The redesigned route is clear of all red and orange areas, but regional escalation (Iran conflict) is unresolved, so the status is **conditional**, not eligible.

## Signature experiences
Lycian Way stages; Kekova sunken city by boat; tent nights at Çıralı and the Chimaera; Termessos and Sagalassos.

## Alternatives considered
- Şanlıurfa, Göbekli Tepe, Mardin: dropped (France orange; near Syria border).
- Mount Nemrut (Adıyaman) and Kaçkar: winter snow and road closure make February–March doubtful; not verified.
- Lake Van and Ani (Kars): cold, remote, and near the Iran border during an unresolved regional conflict; not assessed in detail.
- Istanbul and Cappadocia circuit: not the aim of this proposal.
- Cheaper variant: drop the boat, guide and equipment purchase (economical about EUR 911).

## Open questions and reassessment triggers
Regional escalation or airspace closures; snow at Sagalassos; real flight and car prices; site entrance fees; IDP rule for the participants' licences; visa rules by nationality.
