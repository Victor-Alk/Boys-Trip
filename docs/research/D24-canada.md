# D24 — Canada: The Frozen Saint Lawrence and the Saguenay Fjord

Participant proposal (28 Nov 2026). Researched 28 Nov 2026. Region: Québec (Montréal, Québec City, Charlevoix, Tadoussac, Saguenay, Lac-Saint-Jean).

## Overview
13-day winter road trip, 6–18 Feb 2027, built around the Québec Winter Carnival (5–14 Feb 2027, S06). Montréal (2 nights), rental car to Québec City, Wendake, Hôtel de Glace, Charlevoix, free ferry at Tadoussac, ice-fishing village on the frozen Saguenay, Lac-Saint-Jean, back via Route 175. About 1,500 km of driving.

## Screening verdict
- Seasonality: February excellent (Carnaval, reliable ice); March good.
- Flights: direct CDG–YUL (Air Canada, Air France, Air Transat, S17); fare is an estimate, not quoted.
- Local costs: high; realistic about EUR 2,980 per person (outside budget).
- Security: eligible. Cultural interest: moderate-high (Wendake, Carnaval, Old Québec). Adventure: moderate (dog sledding, snowshoeing, ice fishing). Independent travel: very easy.

## February vs March
Feb: day -7 to -5 C, night -19 to -16 C, 52–64 cm snow (S08, S09). March: day -1 to 0 C, night -12 to -9 C, 49–50 cm snow, thaw risk, ice-fishing cabins end earlier, sugar shacks start, no Carnaval, school break (early March) raises prices. Aurora at 48 N is possible but not planned.

## Itinerary rationale
Carnaval fixes early Feb. Québec City as hub, then the Route 138 coast, the free ferry across the fjord, Route 172 up the fjord. Day 10 is a flexible storm day. Long legs: day 11 (about 290 km) and day 12 (255 km).

## Transport & driving legality
Two licensed drivers share a rental SUV; winter tyres (3PMSF) are mandatory 1 Dec–15 Mar on Québec-registered cars (S10); rentals are normally equipped, confirm. Licence/IDP validity for non-French/English licences not confirmed. No motorcycles or snowmobiles (licence needed); passengers in guided dog sleds only.

## Signature experiences
Carnaval; ice fishing at Saint-Fulgence; dog sledding in Charlevoix; Hôtel de Glace visit.

## Alternatives considered
- Yukon (Whitehorse, aurora, Yukon Quest): not researched in depth; long connecting flights and higher local costs expected.
- Canadian Rockies (Banff/Jasper): ice walks and huts, but high lodging prices and avalanche closures; not researched in depth.
- Cheaper variant: hostels/apartments with kitchens, groceries, one paid activity, 10 days Québec–Charlevoix only: about EUR 2,000–2,100 (economical scenario, 2,089).
