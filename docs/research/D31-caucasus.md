# D31 — Caucasus: Supra, Qvevri and Monasteries Between Two Capitals

Researched 2026-11-28. Participant proposal. Continent set to Asia (Transcaucasia, south of the Greater Caucasus watershed; Georgia and Armenia are usually grouped in Western Asia, though both are Council of Europe members).

## Overview
Thirteen days, open-jaw Paris CDG to Tbilisi, home from Yerevan. Georgia: Tbilisi, Mtskheta, Uplistsikhe, Kazbegi (Gergeti on snowshoes), Kakheti (qvevri cellars, supra, Alaverdi, Sighnaghi). Armenia: Sadakhlo-Bagratashen crossing on foot, Debed canyon (Haghpat, Sanahin), Dilijan, Lake Sevan (north-west shore), Yerevan, Garni and Geghard. Azerbaijan is not included.

## Screening verdict
- Seasonality: fair in February, good in March. Mountain days are weather-dependent; the lowland and city days are robust.
- Flights: direct Paris-Tbilisi is reported by aggregators; schedules for 2027 unverified. Fare is a planning estimate, not a quote.
- Local costs: low to moderate (about €1,070 per person locally, realistic).
- Security: **conditional** (see below).
- Cultural interest: high (UNESCO Mtskheta, qvevri winemaking, Haghpat/Sanahin, Geghard).
- Adventure: moderate; one genuine winter mountain day.
- Independent-travel feasibility: good with hired drivers; self-drive not recommended in winter.

## February vs March
February: Jvari Pass closures are most frequent (sources vary, from 3-5 closures a month for a few hours to multi-day closures between November and March; these are travel-blog and operator figures, not official data). Kazbegi is deeply snow-covered, Gergeti needs snowshoes. March: milder in the lowlands, medium closure risk, snow still at Kazbegi. Recommended window 11-23 March 2027. Ramadan: not relevant. Women's Day (8 March) and Georgian Mother's Day (3 March) are public holidays. Climate figures are approximate normals recalled from general knowledge; no meteorological source was fetched.

## Itinerary rationale
- **Svaneti/Ushguli (winter feasibility check):** Mestia is reachable by road and by weather-dependent Vanilla Sky flights; the winter marshrutka can take up to 10 h, and Ushguli may be impassable without specialist vehicles (sources: georgia.travel Svaneti in winter, Wander-Lush; search-result summaries only). It would consume 3-4 days on top of the Tbilisi-Kakheti-Kazbegi loop and add significant weather risk, so it is dropped.
- **Tusheti:** its road is closed in winter, so it is not an option.
- **Kazbegi:** kept as the single high-mountain stop, with two buffer days (5 and 6) because the Jvari Pass can close.
- **Vardzia and David Gareja:** dropped for time; Vardzia (Samtskhe-Javakheti) needs two days from Tbilisi; David Gareja is in a border area with a long-running Georgia-Azerbaijan boundary dispute and was not assessed in the fetched advisories, so it is an alternative only after a local check.
- **Syunik (Tatev, Wings of Tatev, Goris, Kapan):** excluded; France classes the whole province orange (see below).
- **Noravank:** dropped; Dutch advice warns of mines between Yeraskh and Noravank, and the US allows transit but not stopping at Yeraskh.
- **Ijevan-Noyemberyan road (M16/H26):** avoided; Dilijan is reached from the Debed canyon via Vanadzor.

## Transport and driving legality
Hired drivers for all road legs (Georgia 6 days, Armenia 4 days). The border is crossed on foot at Sadakhlo-Bagratashen with a driver change. Cross-border rental permissions were not verified. UK FCDO: a 1968 IDP is needed in Armenia with a UK licence; Georgian requirements were not confirmed. Two of four hold car licences; no motorcycles. Winter driving over Jvari Pass needs chains/4WD.

## Security assessment (checked 2026-11-28)
- **France Diplomatie, Armenia** (Sécurité text, updated 24 Nov 2026; zones last updated 4 Mar 2026): red (formellement déconseillé): Armenian border zones with Azerbaijan, and the M16 Idjevan-Noyemberian stretch (occasional fire from Azerbaijan); **orange (déconseillé sauf raison impérative): the province of Syunik**; yellow: the rest of the country. This is the decisive constraint: **Tatev, Wings of Tatev, Goris, Kapan are inside Syunik** and are excluded.
- **France Diplomatie, Georgia** (page dated 10 Sept 2026): colour map is an image; the fetched text mentions protests since 28 Nov 2024, power cuts and mandatory health insurance, but no zone colours. I did not invent a level; this remains to be confirmed.
- **UK FCDO, Armenia** (updated 1 Mar 2026): against all travel within 5 km of the eastern Azerbaijan border and on the M16/H26 Ijevan-Noyemberyan road. Syunik/Goris/Kapan/Tatev not named.
- **UK FCDO, Georgia** (updated 12 Nov 2026): against all travel to Abkhazia and South Ossetia; all but essential near the administrative boundary lines; widespread regional travel disruption.
- **Nederland Wereldwijd:** Armenia red within 5 km of the Azerbaijan border, rest yellow (31 Jul 2026); Georgia red for Abkhazia/South Ossetia and border zone, rest yellow (24 Jun 2026).
- **US State Department feed** (fetched): Armenia Level 2 (4 Sep 2025), embassy staff restricted in Gegharkunik east of Vardenis, Syunik east of Goris and south of Kapan; Georgia Level 1 (12 Mar 2025); Azerbaijan Level 3 (27 Apr 2026) citing drone and missile threat from Iran since 28 Feb 2026 and landmines.
- Smartraveller pages timed out and could not be read.

Verdict: **conditional**. The redesigned route avoids all red and orange zones, but the status is conditional because of (1) the Middle East/Iran escalation that FCDO and US advisories say disrupted regional airspace and flights, (2) the unreadable France zone map for Georgia, and (3) unresolved Armenia-Azerbaijan tensions. If the group wants Tatev, it falls in an orange zone and the destination would be excluded.

## Signature experiences
Gergeti Trinity on snowshoes; qvevri cellar and supra in Kakheti; the foot border crossing to the Debed monasteries; Tbilisi sulphur baths.

## Alternatives considered
A cheaper variant: marshrutkas to Kazbegi and Kakheti, about €900 per person. Not recommended: Svaneti in winter, Tusheti, Syunik, Azerbaijan.

## Open questions and reassessment triggers
France Georgia zone map; direct flight availability and fares; Jvari Pass status; cross-border rental policy; IDP requirement in Georgia; any change to FCDO or France Syunik advice; Iran-related airspace restrictions.
