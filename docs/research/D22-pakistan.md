# D22 — Pakistan: research notes

Added at the group's request (origin: participant-proposal, 2026-11-28). Checked 2026-11-28.

## Overview
Concept: Karakoram Highway and Hunza (Gilgit-Baltistan), Lahore (Mughal heritage, food), Taxila and the Salt Range (Rohtas Fort, Khewra), Sindh (Mohenjo-daro, Makli, Indus delta) and Cholistan (Derawar Fort). 13 days, hired driver plus domestic flights. Classified **excluded**; the itinerary is informational.

## Screening verdict
- Seasonality: lowlands pleasant in Feb–Mar; the north is cold, the Khunjerab Pass is generally closed to all vehicles 30 Dec–1 Apr (S05), the Karakoram Highway can close for extended periods in heavy snow (S06), and flights to Skardu and Gilgit are weather dependent (S07 gives routes only; cancellation frequency not verified).
- Flights: CDG–ISB via a Gulf or Turkish hub; France reports many Pakistan–Gulf links suspended (S01). Price is a planning estimate.
- Security: decisive, see below.
- Independent travel: self-drive not recommended; hired driver and guide; NOC rules not verified.

## February vs March
February: Ramadan from about 8 Feb, north snowbound (poor). March: Ramadan ends about 9 March, lowlands warm, Hunza still cold, pass closed until around April (fair).

## Itinerary rationale
Informational only. Because the Karakoram Highway between Mansehra and Chilas is against all travel (S02), the north would have to be reached by air to Gilgit or Skardu. Lahore is the only large-city stop; Sindh and Cholistan would complete the Indus story.

## Transport and driving legality
Hired car with driver; PIA, Airblue and AirSial serve Islamabad–Skardu (S07). No motorcycles; passenger or rickshaw with driver only. IDP and rental rules not verified.

## Security assessment (checked 2026-11-28)
- **France Diplomatie (S01, page 25 Sep 2026; zones 11 Mar 2026):** red (formellement déconseillé): Balochistan, ex-FATA, Khyber-Pakhtunkhwa, Punjab except the Faisalabad–Lahore–Islamabad triangle, Azad Kashmir, Indian border. Rest of country orange (déconseillé sauf raison impérative) including Sindh, Gilgit-Baltistan and the triangle. Terrorism risk significantly raised after Pakistan–Afghanistan clashes since 26 Feb 2026; suicide attacks in Islamabad 11 Nov 2025 and 6 Feb 2026. Read from the PDF export of the page (the web page does not show the zones in text).
- **UK FCDO (S02, updated 18 Nov 2026):** against all travel: KP, Karakoram Highway Mansehra–Chilas, Balochistan, Afghan border and LoC (10 miles); against all but essential: Sindh north of and including Nawabshah (includes Mohenjo-daro), AJK, Dera Ghazi Khan, Indian border (5 miles). Gilgit-Baltistan not listed.
- **Netherlands (S03, 9 Sep 2026):** Islamabad, Lahore, Hunza, Gilgit, Skardu yellow; Sindh and most of Punjab orange.
- **US State (S04):** Level 3, citing armed conflict, terrorism, crime and kidnapping.
- Smartraveller could not be fetched (timeout) and is not cited.
