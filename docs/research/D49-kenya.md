# D49 — Kenya: Four Thousand Metres to the Swahili Coast

Researched 2026-11-28. Participant proposal (28 Nov 2026). Distinct from the Tanzania concept (D48): no Serengeti, Ngorongoro or Kilimanjaro, no Tanzanian leg.

## Overview

A 15-day route (day 1 evening flight, day 15 arrival): Nairobi – Nanyuki – Mount Kenya Sirimon-to-Chogoria traverse to Point Lenana (4,985 m) – Nairobi – Lake Naivasha and Hell's Gate (cycling) – Maasai Mara (2 nights) – Nairobi – Lamu Old Town and Shela by air (2 nights) – Nairobi – Paris. Avoids Samburu/Buffalo Springs, Isiolo, Marsabit, Lamu mainland, Garissa, Tana River, Moyale and the coast north of Malindi. Amboseli, Lake Nakuru, Tsavo and the SGR to Mombasa are not on the route (see alternatives).

## February vs March

- **February (preferred):** Mount Kenya's drier season (December to mid-March), Nairobi about 27/14 C and 36 mm rain, Lamu about 31/25 C and 4 mm. Ramadan starts about 8 February, which affects Lamu (quiet days, modest dress, eating discretion); the trek, Hell's Gate and the Mara are not affected.
- **March:** Nairobi about 28/15 C and 75 mm rain, Lamu about 32/26 C and 25 mm; long rains on the mountain from mid-March and in the Mara from late March or April. Eid al-Fitr about 9–10 March. Fine for the first half only.
- **Ramadan variant:** to avoid Ramadan, start about 10 March (Lamu after Eid) but then the trek runs into the rains; Ramadan itself is not a barrier to visiting Lamu.

## Itinerary rationale

Mount Kenya first because the Sirimon-to-Chogoria traverse is the physical challenge and needs the driest window; the Rift Valley and Mara then give wildlife with little effort; Lamu ends the trip with cultural immersion and warm water. The ascent profile (Nanyuki about 1,950 m, then 3,300 m, 4,200 m, 4,985 m in three days) is faster than recommended: ask the operator for an extra acclimatisation night (it would extend the trip to 16 days or drop Hell's Gate), discuss acetazolamide with a doctor and descend if symptoms worsen. Day 8 (Hell's Gate) and day 11 (Mara–Nairobi) are flexible.

Day plan: 1 flight; 2 Nairobi–Nanyuki; 3 Sirimon Gate–Old Moses; 4 Old Moses–Shipton's; 5 Lenana–Mintos; 6 Mintos–Lake Ellis; 7 Chogoria–Nairobi; 8 Hell's Gate; 9 Naivasha–Mara; 10 Mara; 11 Mara–Nairobi; 12 fly to Lamu; 13 dhow to Shela; 14 fly to Nairobi and Paris; 15 arrival.

## Transport and driving legality

Six vehicle-days with a driver-guide 4x4 (fuel included): Nairobi–Nanyuki, Chogoria–Nairobi, Nairobi–Naivasha/Hell's Gate, Naivasha–Mara, a Mara day, Mara–Nairobi. Self-drive is feasible on tarmac but not recommended: UK FCDO lists carjacking, fake checkpoints, poor driving standards, night driving risk; a UK photocard is valid for 3 months, other licences usually need an IDP. Two of the four hold car licences, so self-drive could be shared, but the Mara tracks, the Laikipia approach and Nairobi traffic favour a driver. Nobody rides a motorcycle (boda-boda). Domestic flights: Jambojet, Safarilink and others Nairobi–Lamu (aggregator snapshot from about 113 EUR one way); Safarilink Wilson–Mara return published at USD 362 (undated). SGR Nairobi–Mombasa economy reported at KSh 1,500 (secondary), not used.

Economical: local 1,911.5 + 10 percent 191 + flights 570 = 2,673. Comfortable: local 3,941 + 15 percent 591 + flights 850 = 5,382. All three are outside the 2,500 EUR limit.

Status: outside budget; classification is robust to price changes. Key assumptions: Mount Kenya USD 70 per day (secondary guide), Hell's Gate USD 50, Mara USD 100 per 24 h January to June (rising to USD 200 from July), Mara conservancy fees not included. The KWS October 2025 fee sheet and Legal Notice No. 160 of 2025 were not readable in this check.

**Cheaper variant (estimate, not priced):** drop the Mara (saves camp, fees, three vehicle-days, community visit: about 570 EUR) for Mount Kenya + Hell's Gate + Lamu in 12 days: about 2,700–2,800 EUR. Still above 2,500 EUR because flights, Mount Kenya park fees and the mandatory insurance are fixed. A trek-only Mount Kenya + Nairobi trip of 9 days could reach about 2,000–2,300 EUR but loses the safari and coast.

## Security assessment (checked 2026-11-28)

- **UK FCDO** (last updated 6 Nov 2026): against all travel to Mandera, parts of Wajir and Garissa, and all of Lamu County except Lamu and Manda islands; against all but essential travel to Garissa Town, Tana River north, the coastal strip between the Tana and Galana rivers, and Moyale. Terrorism threat high; terrorist kidnapping threat high in northern border and coastal counties; Lamu and Manda by air only. Laikipia, Mount Kenya, Mara and Nairobi are not named.
- **France Diplomatie** (page updated 25 Nov 2026; zones updated 25 Sep 2026): red for the Somali border zone to 100 km inland, mainland Lamu, South Sudan and Ethiopia borders; orange for remote West Pokot, Turkana, Marsabit and Isiolo, western Mandera/Wajir/Garissa, the coast north of Malindi and some Nairobi districts; Lamu and Manda islands yellow, access by air only; curfews in parts of Laikipia, Samburu and four other counties. General elections on 10 August 2027; flash floods warning for Oct–Dec; Ebola alert (6 Oct 2026).
- **Netherlands Worldwide** (20 Oct 2026): red for Mandera/Wajir/Garissa, mainland Lamu and Kiwayu; orange for Marsabit/Moyale, Tana Delta and Malindi coast, Mombasa and Nairobi slums; the rest yellow; herder-farmer violence in Laikipia and Samburu.
- **US State Department:** page returned HTTP 403, not read. **Smartraveller:** request timed out, not read.
- **Ebola:** first imported case 6 Oct 2026, nine cases reported linked to a single DRC entry; last patient discharged 11 Nov 2026 and a 42-day countdown began, ending about late December 2026; the DRC Bundibugyo outbreak continues (more than 10,000 confirmed cases reported by Africa CDC).

Classification: **conditional.** The route is outside every advised-against zone and the Lamu islands are excepted by the UK and yellow in France. The status is conditional because of significant national terrorism and kidnapping threats (Lamu was the site of the 2020 Manda Bay attack), an Ebola outbreak not yet declared over, curfews in Laikipia and Samburu next to the Mount Kenya approach, and the pre-election situation. A Lamu-free route (replace with Amboseli and Tsavo or a Nairobi extension) would likely classify as eligible; Samburu and Buffalo Springs were left out because of Isiolo/Samburu curfews and herder violence.

## Signature experiences

1. **Point Lenana at sunrise (Sirimon to Chogoria):** 4 nights; package about 360, fees about 305, hut and gear about 95 EUR; guide, cook and porters; permit via KWS fees.
2. **Hell's Gate cycling and gorge walk:** entry about 44 EUR, cycling and guide about 35 EUR; guide compulsory in the gorge.
3. **Mara dry-season game drives:** about 560 EUR; resident Big Five, no migration (July–October); Maasai community visit paid directly.
4. **Lamu Old Town and dhow:** about 330 EUR including flights; UNESCO World Heritage (2001).

## Alternatives considered

- **Amboseli (Kilimanjaro views):** KWS fee about USD 90 per day; 4 h drive from Nairobi; could replace Lamu if the group wants lower security risk.
- **Lake Nakuru:** USD 90 per day; skipped to keep the days uncrowded.
- **Samburu/Buffalo Springs, Laikipia conservancies:** northern specials but in the curfew counties and near Isiolo (orange in France); not on the route.
- **Watamu/Diani:** beach resorts, outside the expedition identity; the coast north of Malindi is orange.
- **Madaraka Express (SGR) to Mombasa:** possible but Mombasa and the Tana coast bring more crime and security caveats.
- **Naro Moru route:** shorter but the Vertical Bog; Sirimon–Chogoria is the requested option.
