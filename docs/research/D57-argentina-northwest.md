# D57 — Argentina, Northwest: Carnival of the Devils in the Painted Quebrada

Researched 28 Nov 2026 (origin: participant proposal, 28 Nov 2026). Second Argentina route after D29 (Patagonia). Planning figures are estimates unless stated.

## Overview
A 13-day self-drive loop from Salta: Quebrada de Humahuaca (Purmamarca, Tilcara, Humahuaca, Hornocal) at carnival time, a night in Iruya, a dawn on the Salinas Grandes, then the Cuesta del Obispo, Cachi, RN40 and Cafayate and the Quebrada de las Conchas back to Salta. About 1,450 km, with 3 camping nights. It does not duplicate D20 (Atacama): the angle is village festival, Andean culture and valleys, not the empty high desert.

## Screening verdict
- **Seasonality:** rainy season. Salta 147 mm in February and 107 mm in March; Humahuaca 48 and 26 mm (1961-70). Landslides on the Iruya road, the Cuesta de Lipán (a km-46 slide reported in a past rain), RP33 and RN68 are the main operational risk. The Dutch advice says north-west roads are sometimes impassable Dec-Mar.
- **Flights:** CDG-EZE reused from D29 (realistic EUR 1,050, representative), plus AEP-SLA return about EUR 105 (ARS 117,000-239,000 snapshots at about ARS 1,700 per euro).
- **Costs:** realistic EUR 2,434 per person (validator): flights 1,050 + local 1,236 + contingency 148. Exceptional expenditure (EUR 2,001-2,500).
- **Security:** eligible (see below).
- **Cultural interest:** high in February (carnival), moderate in March.
- **Adventure:** moderate; altitude and gravel are the challenge, not technical skills.
- **Independent-travel feasibility:** good; two drivers, daylight only.

Honest take: without the carnival this route competes poorly with D20 and D18; with it, it is distinctive. If the group values road certainty and cost more, choose March.

## Itinerary rationale
Gradual acclimatisation: Salta 1,150 m (days 2-3), Purmamarca 2,200 m, Tilcara 2,460 m, Humahuaca 3,012 m, then the Hornocal viewpoint near 4,350 m on day 5 and the Salinas Grandes (about 3,450 m; pass about 4,170 m, not verified) on day 8. Carnival nights are days 3 to 6; Iruya (day 6) falls on Carnival Monday. Flexible days: 7 (Iruya buffer) and 10 (RN40 gravel). Cachi and Cafayate come after the Quebrada, when rain risk on RP33 and RN40 can be checked.

## Transport and driving legality
Self-drive with the two licence holders; IDP recommended; no motorcycles for anyone unlicensed (alternatives: hired driver, minibus tours, buses). No night driving (France Diplomatie). Rental from Salta airport (Enterprise, Hertz listed); the contract must allow Jujuy, gravel and a second driver. No one-way fee. Gravel: Hornocal (RP73, about 25 km), Iruya (about 50 km), RP33 last stretch to Cachi (about 12 km), parts of RN40.

Economical EUR 1,790 (within preferred budget); comfortable EUR 4,011 (outside budget: 4x4, driver, rooms every night).
Iguazu add-on (not budgeted): park ticket ARS 60,000 (about EUR 35), flights via Buenos Aires EUR 150-250, two nights and transfers: roughly EUR 300-450 per person and 2 extra days, so within 15 days only by cutting Cachi and Cafayate.

## Security assessment (checked 28 Nov 2026)
- France Diplomatie country page (updated 2 Nov 2026): no mention of Salta, Jujuy, the north-west or the Bolivian border; alerts El Nino (2 Nov), enhanced vigilance (10 Sep), measles (13 Mar). Securite page (15 Sep): road violence, no night driving, altitude acclimatisation; floods named for other provinces.
- UK FCDO safety page: no warning; earthquake risk in Salta and Jujuy; altitude sickness; northern provinces flood; express kidnappings in cities.
- Nederland Wereldwijd: yellow (updated 25 Nov 2026); north-west floods Dec-Mar, roads sometimes impassable.
- Smartraveller (via search snippet, page timed out): normal precautions (11 Nov 2026), higher level for Rosario.
- US State Department: HTTP 403, not read.

Classification: **eligible**. Carnival adds drunk driving and crowds, not conflict risk.

## Signature experiences
Carnival in Tilcara and Humahuaca; Hornocal; Salinas Grandes; Iruya; Cuesta del Obispo to Cafayate. Respect: ask before photographing dancers and offerings, buy from community stalls, be sensitive about the lithium dispute.

## Alternatives considered
March variant (about EUR 70 cheaper on rooms); adding Iguazu; a route into Chile towards San Pedro (overlaps D20, border logistics); La Quiaca / Bolivian border (not included).
