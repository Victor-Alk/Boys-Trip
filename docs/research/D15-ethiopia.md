# D15 — Ethiopia: Rock Churches and the Roof of Africa

Research date: 2026-11-27. Status: **excluded (security)**, informational itinerary only.

## Overview
Concept: ancient civilisations, living Orthodox heritage and highland landscapes — Lalibela (eleven rock-hewn churches, UNESCO 1978), Gondar's royal enclosure, Lake Tana, the Simien escarpment, Axum and the Gheralta cliff churches, the Danakil, and a southern/eastern variant (Bale Mountains, Harenna Forest, Harar, Addis Ababa).

## February vs March
February preferred: drier, clearer, before Great Lent. Expected start of Abiy Tsom about 8 March 2027 (computed: Orthodox Easter 2 May 2027 minus 55 days; not verified from an official source). Adwa Victory Day 2 March. Ramadan (~8 Feb–9 Mar) affects Harar and eastern Ethiopia only.

## Transport & driving legality
Ethiopian Airlines domestic flights connect the north but may be cancelled at short notice; flights to Tigray suspended since 23 Sept 2026 (France Diplomatie). Self-drive not recommended (poor road safety, no night driving, roadblocks, fuel shortages per FCDO). Hired 4x4 with driver. IDP may be required; no motorcycle riding without a licence.

Bale and Harar: no specific advice.
