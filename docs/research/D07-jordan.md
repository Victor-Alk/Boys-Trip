# D07 — Jordan: "Nabataean Stone, Bedouin Sand and the Dana Descent"

Research agent A07. Checked 2026-11-27. Data file: `data/destinations/D07-jordan.json`.

## Overview

13 days (12 nights), 12–24 March 2027: Amman → Jerash → Madaba/Mount Nebo → Dead Sea → Dana → (guided walk) Feynan → Shobak → Wadi Musa (3 nights, Petra, Little Petra, back trail) → Wadi Rum (2 nights, Bedouin camp + remote night) → Desert Highway → Amman. The route stays out of Aqaba, Azraq, Mafraq, the north-eastern desert castles, Umm Qais, Karak city, Ma'an city and the 10 km Syria/Iraq border strips.

## February vs March

- **February:** within Ramadan (≈ 8 Feb–9 Mar). Daytime food outlets partly shut, shorter office hours (Petra News Agency cites 09:00–14:30 for government), Petra by Night uncertain. Amman highs 13–16 °C, lows 5–6 °C; ~48–60 mm rain; Petra's wettest months are Jan–Feb; Wadi Rum can go to 0 °C or below at night (-2 °C recorded Feb 2017 per a travel source); flash floods in wadis November–March (FCDO); Wadi Mujib closed in winter.
- **March:** Eid al-Fitr ≈ 9–11 March (moon-sighting dependent) with domestic holiday crowds, so the window starts 12 March. Amman ~18/7 °C; Petra and Rum comfortable. Rain lower (~30 mm Amman).
- Climate figures are from Rough Guides and aggregators; Jordan Meteorological Department normals were not reachable. No 2027 forecast is implied.

## Itinerary rationale

Front-loaded culture and a gradual climb in altitude to Dana, then a walking transect (Dana → Feynan) as the centre piece. Three nights at Wadi Musa give a full Petra day and a back-trail day with a rain-swap option. Two Rum nights give a Bedouin camp and one remote night with a guide. Day 12 is a flexible buffer. Wadi Mujib Siq Trail is closed in winter (reserve closes every winter for flash floods; reopening is announced in spring, no 2027 date found), so it is not planned. Desert castles (Azraq area) and Umm Qais dropped for security reasons.

## Transport and driving legality

- Rental SUV for four from Amman on day 3 to day 12 (10 days). Two drivers share (additional driver on contract). No motorcycles; Careem/taxi, Bedouin jeeps and lodge 4×4 used otherwise.
- IDP: sources conflict (Sixt requires one only for non-Roman licences; others say Jordan's Convention membership makes it a legal requirement). Carry an IDP if the home licence country issues one. Rental brands at AMM: Hertz, Sixt, Enterprise (listings checked; no live quotes).
- King's Highway vs Desert Highway: this plan uses the Dead Sea Highway/Route 65 and the King's Highway from Dana (avoids Karak, site of a church shooting on 23 Oct 2026) and the Desert Highway for the return (bypasses Ma'an, but no stops there).
- Wadi Rum: self-drive 4×4 in the protected area is restricted (permit regime reported at 25–35 JOD by a secondary guide, unverified); use Bedouin jeeps.
