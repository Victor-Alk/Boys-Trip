## 3. Proposals for the group (to be confirmed by A00)

1. **For Iraq (D02) — Uzbekistan, March 2027.** Same "ancient civilisations and desert" brief, US Level 1 and no FCDO
   restriction, comfortably within €2,000.
2. **For Ethiopia (D15) — Nepal, March 2027.** Highland walking with living religious heritage; within €2,000; the
   compulsory local guide or porter (FCDO) fits the group's no-specialist-skills profile.
3. **For Saudi Arabia (D06) — Cape Verde, February or March 2027.** Volcanic landscapes and long walks with the
   cleanest advisory profile on the list; within €2,000.
4. **For Chile (D20) — Northern Argentina (March), only if a costed brief lands at or below €2,500;** otherwise
   Malaysian Borneo (Sarawak) as a different-landscape alternative.
5. **Standby** if Jordan, Algeria and Egypt remain conditional at the February 2027 recheck: a second desert brief is
   not needed while Tunisia (D08) and the Uzbekistan proposal cover the theme.

Not proposed: Bangladesh (France orange country-wide), Cambodia and Nicaragua (would be conditional under the rule set
used for Jordan), Benin (north excluded), The Gambia (duplicates Senegal), Costa Rica (budget), Guizhou and Karnataka
(same countries as D03 and D04).
