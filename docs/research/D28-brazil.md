# D28 — Brazil: Afro-Bahia and the Valley of Pati

Research date: 2026-11-28. Origin: participant proposal (28 Nov 2026). All local prices are planning estimates unless marked; EUR at about R$ 6.2 (estimate).

## Overview

A 15-day Bahia expedition: Salvador (Afro-Brazilian culture) and the Recôncavo river towns (Cachoeira/São Félix), then a bus west to Lençóis and a five-day guided crossing of the Vale do Pati in the Chapada Diamantina National Park (four nights in valley families' homes), a cave day, and a ferry day before the overnight flight. No car, no camping. Chosen over the Lençóis Maranhenses lagoons (lagoons fill Jan–Jun but access and heat were not researched; best Jun–Sep per common guidance, not verified here), the Pantanal/Bonito and an Amazon riverboat (FCDO advises against all but essential travel on river routes in western Amazonas State; Amazon also not coherent for February). Boipeba/Itacaré was dropped for time: the Morro de São Paulo catamaran takes about 2 h 20 min each way (not on the route) and the Pati needs five days.

## February vs March

- Salvador INMET normals 1991–2020: February 98.7 mm, max about 31.1 °C, min 24.0 °C; March 147.3 mm, 30.9/24.1 °C (S11).
- Lençóis (Climatempo): February 19–29 °C, about 58 mm; March 19–29 °C, about 71 mm (S10). A park-wide dataset from another site gave about 185–193 mm, so the figures conflict; the plateau is wetter than the town.
- The Pati operator recommends mid-March to early June as the low season and says January–May waterfalls are full, trails muddier, rain gear mandatory (S06). The blog source says November–March is rainy with slippery trails (S07).
- Carnival: Sat 6 – Tue 9 Feb, Ash Wednesday 10 Feb; Salvador's programme about 4–10 Feb (S15). Recommended window 13–26 Feb. El Niño is expected from late 2026 into 2027 (S02, S03): heavy rain in the south, drought and heat in the north and north-east. Effect on Bahia is not predicted; unverified.
- Easter 28 Mar 2027 (after the window). Ramadan and Lunar New Year: not relevant.

## Itinerary rationale

Two Salvador days and a Recôncavo day establish culture first; the bus (about 315 km, 6–6.5 h) crosses to Lençóis; day 5 acclimatises; days 6–10 are the Mamut-style Lençóis–Andaraí crossing (day lengths 12, 22, 15, 10, 13 km, S06); day 11 is a flexible cave day; day 12 returns to Salvador; day 13 is a flexible ferry day; day 14 overnight flight, day 15 arrival. The trek follows the operator's published 5-day plan; the group would book with an accredited operator rather than improvise (ICMBio warns the trails are not for improvisation; S08).

## Transport and driving legality

Buses (Rápido Federal about R$ 144+ one way, S14), driver day hires, the operator's transfers and the Salvador–Mar Grande ferry (S20). Self-drive is legal but not planned. IDP requirement was not verified; two of four hold licences. No motorcycles/motorcycle taxis.

## Security assessment

Checked 2026-11-28.

- UK FCDO (S02, S03): advises against all but essential travel to parts of Brazil only (river routes in western Amazonas State). Notes robbery, express kidnapping, arrastões, carjacking, high road accident rate, favela warning, El Niño risk; terrorism threat generic with a 13 Nov 2024 explosion in Brasília.
- Nederland Wereldwijd (S05): yellow for Brazil overall (advice dated 25 Aug 2026, valid to 28 Nov 2026); Salvador/Bahia not mentioned.
- France Diplomatie (S01): the landing page fetched showed alerts (storms in the south, updated 23 Nov 2026; El Niño, 29 Oct 2026; measles); the colour-coded security map and the Sécurité sub-page were not retrievable as text. The level for Bahia is therefore NOT verified. The US State Department (403) and Smartraveller (timeout) pages could not be fetched.
- Classification: eligible. Ordinary crime is not conflict, terrorism or targeted kidnapping; express kidnapping is opportunistic and mitigated by licensed/app rides. Reassess after reading the French map directly.

## Signature experiences

Pati five-day crossing; Afro-Bahian Salvador on foot with capoeira; Cachoeira and São Félix; cave day from Lençóis. Terreiro visits only through local guides and with their rules.

## Alternatives considered

- Carnival-week variant (3–16 Feb): higher prices and crowds, a different experience.
- Shorter trek (3 days, R$ 1,300–2,300, S07): cuts about €250 and reduces risk.
- Morro de São Paulo/Boipeba coast after the Chapada: not included; the catamaran trip is 2 h 20 min each way.
- Lençóis Maranhenses, Pantanal, Amazon: not chosen (see Overview).
