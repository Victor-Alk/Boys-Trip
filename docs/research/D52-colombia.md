# D52 — Colombia: Lost City, Antioquia and the Coffee Axis

Researched 2026-11-28 (participant proposal of the same date). Status: **security conditional**, realistic **€2,376 per person** (exceptional expenditure band), best window **20 February – 5 March 2027**.

## Overview
A 14-day, no-self-drive route: Santa Marta (with a Tayrona boat day), a licensed four-day trek to Ciudad Perdida (Teyuna) in the Sierra Nevada de Santa Marta, Minca, a flight to Medellín (Comuna 13 with a resident guide, Guatapé), then Salento and the Cocora valley before flying home from Pereira.

## Screening verdict
- **Seasonality:** Dry season in the Sierra Nevada and the Andes (December–March). Window set after the usual 1–15 February Tayrona closure and Barranquilla Carnival (6–9 Feb 2027) and before Holy Week (21–28 March 2027; Monday 22 March is the San José holiday).
- **Flights:** Search snippets: Air France CDG–BOG return €686 for 4–17 March 2027; Lufthansa €590 (14–31 March). No quote for 20 Feb. Realistic €700 (representative).
- **Local costs:** Moderate; the trek (COP 1.86–2.15 million) dominates.
- **Security:** Conditional (below).
- **Cultural interest:** Very high (Tairona archaeology in living Indigenous territory, Comuna 13).
- **Adventure:** Strenuous four-day trek; guide compulsory.
- **Independent-travel feasibility:** Trek only through licensed operators; no self-drive.

## February vs March
February (late): dry, hot coast, firmer trails; risk is the early-month Tayrona closure and Carnival. March: rain returns in the Andes from mid-month; Holy Week crowds from 21 March. Window straddles the two (Either).

## Itinerary rationale
Hardest part first, while legs are fresh. Flights replace 10+ hour road legs; one 6–7 h hired-driver day (Guatapé–Salento) is the longest and has a fallback. Flexible days: D2 (Tayrona closed) and D12 (Cocora rain).

## Transport and driving legality
Self-drive possible but not planned (FCDO: poor driving standards, night risk, blockades). French licence is normally accepted for tourists; IDP advised, not verified from a primary source. No motorcycle riding; Minca and many towns have motorcycle taxis, which the group does not use.

## Security assessment (checked 2026-11-28)
- **UK FCDO** (S01–S03; updated 23 Nov 2026): against all but essential travel to Norte de Santander, Arauca, parts of Chocó and Valle del Cauca, Urabá and Bajo Cauca (Antioquia), southern Bolívar, Guaviare, Cauca except Popayán, parts of Putumayo/Caquetá/Meta (Ariari, except Caño Cristales), and 5 km of the Venezuelan and Ecuadorian borders. Magdalena, Medellín, Guatapé, Quindío and Risaralda are not listed. Lost City only with an organised tour. Express kidnappings common; terrorism "very likely" to be attempted; election violence; scopolamine.
- **France Diplomatie** (S04; updated 3 Nov 2026): the colour map could not be read from the fetched text. Alerts: Puracé area (Cauca) in the red zone and on volcano orange alert; military operations in most red zones (8 Oct 2026); scopolamine alert. **Department colours for Magdalena/Antioquia/Quindío are unverified.**
- **US State Department / Smartraveller:** pages not retrievable (HTTP 403 / timeout). News reports a Level 3 US advisory; unverified. A 24 Sept 2026 US alert for Santa Marta and Route 90 was reported (S05).
- **Armed groups:** In September 2026 the Autodefensas Conquistadores de la Sierra Nevada (ACSN) declared a 72-hour armed strike with blockades and curfew around Santa Marta; 1,500+ police and soldiers deployed; airport operated; reported ended about 25 Sept. June 2026 fighting in the Sierra Nevada stranded tourists and closed the Troncal del Caribe (news). Tayrona closed in Feb 2026 (Kogui dispute); land accesses at Calabazo and Zaino–Cañaveral still closed in June 2026.
- **Elections:** Presidential runoff 21 June 2026, de la Espriella won by about 1 point; inauguration scheduled 7 Aug (not confirmed). No national election in the window; regional elections are due October 2027.
- **Classification: conditional.** No stop sits in an official against-travel area, but the Santa Marta/Sierra Nevada section has unresolved armed-group violence in 2026 and the French map is unverified. Escalate to excluded if advisories change; fallback is the Medellín–Coffee Axis variant.

## Signature experiences
Trek to Teyuna (€520); Comuna 13 with a resident guide (€25); Cocora loop and coffee farm (€30); Tayrona by boat if open (€60).

## Alternatives considered
- **Caño Cristales:** excluded — closed or poor in Feb–Mar (opens roughly June–November) and in the FCDO Ariari warning.
- **Mompox:** southern Bolívar is under FCDO advice and the road from Cartagena is long; left out.
- **San Agustín/Tierradentro:** Huila/Cauca, long roads beside the Cauca red zone; left out.
- **Cartagena and Palomino:** Cartagena adds a long coastal road through the Route 90 corridor; Palomino sits on the disrupted Route 90. Left out.
- **Tatacoa, Los Nevados:** extra road legs and volcano-alert closures (Los Nevados closed Nov 2025); left out.
