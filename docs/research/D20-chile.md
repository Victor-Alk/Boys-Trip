# D20 — Chile: Salt, Steam and the Driest Sky

Checked 2026-11-27. Source ids refer to `data/destinations/D20-chile.json`. Prices are planning estimates unless stated.

## Overview

A 13-day self-drive (4–16 March 2027) around San Pedro de Atacama, Antofagasta Region: Pukará de Quitor, Laguna Chaxa, Toconao, the altiplanic lagoons of Miscanti and Miñiques with Piedras Rojas, Valle de la Luna, El Tatio (4,300 m), the Atacameño village of Caspana, Chiu Chiu and Chuquicamata, then Salar de Tara with a guide. Base: San Pedro (2,400 m). Fly CDG–SCL–Calama, 4x4 pickup rented and returned at Calama. No border crossings.

## Screening verdict

- **Seasonality:** fair in February, good in March (altiplanic winter; see below).
- **Flights:** long and expensive; estimate €1,150 realistic for CDG–SCL return (not observed).
- **Local costs:** high (San Pedro is Chile's priciest rural base); about €1,700 pp with contingency in the realistic scenario.
- **Security:** eligible (France Diplomatie and UK FCDO, no red/orange or against-travel area on the route).
- **Cultural interest:** moderate to good (Lickanantay heritage, Quitor, Caspana, Chiu Chiu).
- **Adventure:** high altitude, gravel 4x4, geysers, astronomy.
- **Independent travel:** feasible with two drivers.
- **Budget:** realistic €2,850 pp, **outside budget (>€2,500), not eligible**. Economical scenario €1,949.

## February vs March

- Altiplanic ("Bolivian") winter: summer convective storms on the altiplano peak in January and February and usually decline in March. This is an established seasonal pattern, **not verified on a fetched page** (the Wikipedia article I tried was a 404) and there is no 2027 forecast. Storms can wash out tracks, flood ravines and close sites (El Tatio road, lagoons, Tara, Valle de la Luna).
- San Pedro normals (S07): Feb 28.0/9.4 C, 21 mm; Mar 27.0/7.9 C, 7 mm. Altiplano nights near or below 0 C are an estimate; El Tatio is ~4,320 m with up to 40 C daily swings (S09).
- Moon (standard astronomical tables, not fetched): new moons 6 Feb and 8 Mar 2027; full moons 20 Feb and 22 Mar. Darkest skies: 1–12 Feb or 4–12 Mar. March is chosen mainly on storm risk; Feb's 6 Feb new moon is equally good for the sky.
- Demand: Chilean school holidays make Jan–Feb peak. Easter is 28 Mar 2027 (after the window). Ramadan and Lunar New Year: not relevant.
- Flights: higher in Feb, lower in March; unverified.

## Itinerary rationale

Acclimatise two nights at 2,400 m with low-altitude days (Quitor, Chaxa, Toconao), then Miscanti at 4,100–4,350 m (day 5), El Tatio and Caspana (day 7), and Tara (day 10). Days 6 and 11 are weather buffers; day 6 holds Valle de la Luna. Caspana gives a community stay; Chuquicamata gives the mining counterpoint. Camping 5 nights in San Pedro, rooms 5 nights. Reached via the Calama–San Pedro road (about 100 km).

North (Putre/Lauca, Arica) and south (Pan de Azúcar, Copiapó, Ojos del Salado) loops were considered: each needs 800–1,200 km extra driving or domestic flights and lengthens an already-costly trip; Putre/Lauca also sits near the Peru/Bolivia border strips where the advisories warn about landmines. Rejected.

## Transport & driving legality

Rent a 4x4 or pickup in Calama (cheaper than San Pedro); fuel at San Pedro is scarce, fill up before remote days (S06, S13, S14). A credit card, licence and passport are needed (S12). IDP recommended if the licence is not in Spanish; FCDO says UK photocard is valid for three months (S04). Two of four hold licences; both must be named on the contract. Not verified: minimum age, deposits, insurance exclusions on unpaved roads. No motorcycles. Driving at altitude: engine power and brakes degrade; flat tyres are common.

## Security assessment

- France Diplomatie (S01, S02), checked 2026-11-27, last updated 2 Nov 2026: yellow "vigilance renforcée" only for Araucanía, Biobío, Los Lagos and Easter Island; Antofagasta not listed. Landmines near Peru, Bolivia and Argentina borders (regions 1, 2, 5, 12), some old or displaced; thefts and rental-car attacks including Calama and San Pedro. El Niño vigilance alert 2 Nov 2026.
- UK FCDO (S03–S05), updated 9 Nov 2026: no area advised against; region-specific advice only for Araucanía; landmines in regions XV, I, II; Lascar volcano eruption risk; protests in Santiago; 4WD for rural areas.
- Netherlands (page returned no advice), Smartraveller (timed out) and US State Dept (HTTP 403) could not be read, so were not used.
- Classification: **eligible**. Route avoids borders; Tara is the closest, so guide and marked tracks. Not a conflict region.

## Signature experiences

El Tatio at dawn; Miscanti and Piedras Rojas; Chaxa flamingos plus a new-moon astronomy tour; a night in Caspana; Salar de Tara with a guide. ALMA public visits (Operations Support Facility at 2,900 m, weekend mornings, registration; page dated 2020, S10) are optional and unverified for 2027; the 5,000 m array is closed to the public.

## Alternatives considered

Cheaper variant (credible, shown by the economical scenario, €1,949): fly via Madrid or São Paulo (about €850), shorter 10-day stay with 8 rental days, camp more, own sleeping bags, cook, one fewer paid activity. This still includes the lagoons, Tatio and Chaxa, but the unguided Tara day is not recommended; swap it for a guided shared tour. Realistic version cannot be brought under €2,500 without these cuts. Also considered: ALMA/Cerro Toco and Licancabur summits (guide, fitness, permits; not planned), Uyuni crossing (border, 3 days, excluded).

## Open questions / reassessment triggers
