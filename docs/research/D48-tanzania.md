# D48 — Tanzania: Calving Plains, Crater Rim and the Mountain of God

Researched 2026-11-28 (participant proposal). Realistic total per person about EUR 3,600 including flights: outside budget. Security: conditional.

## Overview

A 14-day mobile-camping loop in northern Tanzania timed for the February wildebeest calving season: Arusha, Tarangire, Mto wa Mbu, Lake Eyasi (Hadzabe and Datoga visits via the cultural tourism programme), Ngorongoro rim and crater, Ndutu, Seronera (central Serengeti), Olduvai Gorge, Engaruka ruins and Lake Natron with a night climb of Ol Doinyo Lengai. Eleven camping nights. The concept stays in Tanzania and does not cross into Kenya.

## Screening verdict

- Seasonality: February excellent (peak calving; long rains usually start in March). March fair.
- Flights: CDG-JRO about EUR 610-950 return in fare-page snapshots; no quote for the dates, so representative EUR 880.
- Local costs: very high. Serengeti USD 82.60, Ngorongoro USD 70.80, Tarangire USD 59 per 24 h (2026 operator tables, incl. 18% VAT), crater service fee USD 295 per vehicle, NCAA campsites USD 59 pp per night, private camping safari market USD 250-400 pp per day.
- Security: conditional (see below).
- Cultural interest: moderate to good (Olduvai, Eyasi, Mto wa Mbu, Engaruka). Adventure: good (Lengai night climb, camping, ranger walk). Independent-travel feasibility: self-drive possible but rare; guided mobile camping is realistic.

## February vs March

- February: about 80% of calving within 2-3 weeks, herds concentrated on the Ndutu plains, predators active, showers sporadic (Horizon Guides). Tarangire is green and quieter. Tracks mostly passable. High demand: Ndutu campsites and crews book out early.
- March: calving continues to about mid-March; long rains build, afternoon showers, depleted grazing, herds begin moving north-west. Muddy tracks (Ndutu, Engaruka-Natron) and Lengai ash are harder. Slightly lower demand. Easter 26-29 March 2027.
- Climate figures in the JSON are generic ranges, not station tables; no 2027 forecast exists. El Niño alert (France Diplomatie, 2 Nov 2026) adds flooding risk for early 2027.

## Itinerary rationale

Tarangire first (acclimatise, 2.5 h from Arusha), then Mto wa Mbu and Eyasi (culture), the rim for a dawn crater descent, Ndutu for two calving days, Seronera for leopard country, Olduvai on the way back, Engaruka and Natron for the volcano. Day 9 and day 12 are flexible. Usambara highland walking was dropped (8+ h driving from Arusha; no slot); a ranger-led walk in the Ngorongoro highlands (Olmoti) replaces it.

Kilimanjaro: 7-day Machame park fees about USD 956 incl. VAT (USD 70/day conservation, 50/night camping, 20 rescue, +18% VAT) plus operator fees (not retrieved from a source; typically USD 1,500-2,500). Does not fit with the safari in 14 days and would push the total to about EUR 4,000+. Offered only as a separate variant. Zanzibar/Stone Town: dropped (domestic flight or ferry, additional days, FCDO crime warning for Stone Town and beaches, and mandatory Zanzibar insurance via ZIC).

## Transport and driving legality

Core: private 4x4 pop-up-roof Land Cruiser with driver-guide and cook. Self-drive: 4x4 with rooftop tents from Arusha firms about USD 140-160/day; Serengeti and Natron are restricted for small SUVs. Two of the four hold car licences; the others cannot drive. IDP requirement for Tanzania was not verified from an official source. Left-hand driving, poor road standards, no night driving. Nobody rides boda-bodas without a licence (FCDO also notes poor safety records for them); alternatives are the driver-guide vehicle, taxis and bicycle tours.

## Security assessment (checked 2026-11-28)

- France Diplomatie: no colour for the north other than yellow (vigilance renforcée); red for Mtwara and the Mozambique border; orange for Kagera, Kigoma, Lindi, Ruvuma. Page of 12 Nov 2026 warns of demonstrations since the 2025 election, possible curfews and partial or total internet cuts, fuel/food shortages, no current curfew.
- UK FCDO (updated 30 Oct 2026): against all but essential travel only within 20 km of the Cabo Delgado border. The post-election unrest of 29 Oct 2025 caused deaths and injuries; further demonstrations possible; internet restrictions have been used; posting about politics online can lead to detention.
- Netherlands (27 Nov 2026): yellow; orange for Mtwara.
- Smartraveller: "Exercise a high degree of caution" (index entry 2 Oct 2026); page fetch timed out.
- US State Department: Level 3 Reconsider travel reported since 31 Oct 2025; the official page returned 403, so the current level is unconfirmed.
- Classification: conditional (conflicting tone between the US and European advice; unresolved unrest risk; unconfirmed US level). The route avoids all red and orange zones; a clear improvement or US downgrade would support eligible.

## Signature experiences

Calving season on the Ndutu plains; Ngorongoro Crater at dawn; Ol Doinyo Lengai at night; Lake Eyasi (consent-based Hadzabe visit, agreed fees; sources on fees conflict, USD 10 per party to about USD 110 per vehicle).

## Alternatives considered

Kilimanjaro variant; Zanzibar extension; Usambara walking; self-drive economical scenario; a shorter Ndutu-only loop to cut costs; Kenya is covered separately and not combined.
