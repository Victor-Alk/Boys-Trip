# D04 — India, Northeast: research notes

Date checked: 2026-11-27. Agent A04. Slug `india`.

## Overview
"India — The Khasi Forests and the Brahmaputra's Islands": a 15-day loop (6–20 Feb 2027) Guwahati → Mawphlang/Laitlum → Nartiang → Hajo/Sualkuchi → Nameri → Kaziranga → Majuli → Charaideo/Sivasagar → Dibrugarh. The route is designed to be unusual even for travellers who know India: community homestays, a foothill jungle camp, a 2024 UNESCO burial landscape and a river island with working monasteries (satras). Hired driver throughout.

## Screening verdict
- **Seasonality:** good in February (dry, clear hills, Kaziranga/Nameri season), good in March (warmer, pre-monsoon storms).
- **Flights:** CDG–DEL return planning estimate €640 (estimate, no live quote) plus separate domestic legs.
- **Local costs:** moderate; realistic €1,155 local + €139 contingency per person.
- **Security:** eligible on the redesigned route (see below).
- **Cultural interest / adventure:** high cultural interest; moderate adventure (walks, rafting, safaris).
- **Independent travel:** hired-driver model, no self-drive; no PAP needed.

## February vs March
Feb: day 16–25 °C, night 5–12 °C, low rain, valley fog. Mar: day 20–29 °C, night 9–17 °C, rising humidity, storms late. Ranges are approximate normals, not from a single cited station. Ali-Ai-Ligang (Mising festival) falls on the first Wednesday of Phagun: 18 Feb 2026 (source S17), so about 17 Feb 2027 is inferred — unconfirmed. Ramadan starts about 8 Feb (limited impact). Losar/Sekrenyi/Nyokum are outside the route; Sekrenyi (about 25 Feb, Nagaland) and Nyokum (about 26 Feb, Arunachal) are usual dates, not verified for 2027. Holi about 22 Mar.

## Itinerary rationale
Days 3–5 Meghalaya (Mawphlang sacred forest, Laitlum, Nartiang); 6–7 Hajo/Sualkuchi to Nameri; 8 Nameri walk and rafting; 9–10 Kaziranga; 11–12 Majuli; 13 Charaideo/Sivasagar to Dibrugarh; 14 flexible; 15 arrival Paris. Longest drives: Day 6 (~5 h), Day 7 (~5.5 h).

## Transport and driving legality
FCDO (S04): UK licence plus 1949 IDP required; high road death rate; avoid night/fog. Self-drive not recommended. Hired car with driver (Innova-class) Guwahati–Dibrugarh; Meghalaya taxi unions limit outside cabs for local sightseeing. Domestic flights DEL–GAU and DIB–DEL. Motorcycles: nobody rides without the legal licence; alternatives are jeep, hired driver, pillion with licensed guide. Dibrugarh was not in the e-Visa entry-airport list, so it is used only for departure.

## Permits and entry
- e-Tourist visa (S09, S10): 30-day US$25 July–March, bank charge 2.5–3%, apply 4–120 days ahead, nationality dependent.
- Not on route: Arunachal PAP (S13: USD 30, approved local operator; applications through operators; validity/group rules inconsistent across sources). Nagaland PAP mandatory via e-FRRO (S11, Feb 2026) except a reported relaxation 26 Nov–15 Dec (S12, a single news source). Mizoram, Manipur: registration with FRO within 24 h; PAP reimposed Dec 2024 per alerts. Sikkim not researched in depth (RAP/PAP; excluded as Dutch orange China-border area).
- Meghalaya: 2026 reports of an ILP-style visitor registration (S14); foreigners' treatment unclear. Allowance in budget line b14.

## Security assessment (checked 2026-11-27)
- FCDO (S01, updated 7 Oct 2026): all but essential travel to Manipur; all travel J&K and 10 km India–Pakistan border; no advice against Assam, Meghalaya, Nagaland, Arunachal, Mizoram, Sikkim. Regional risks (S02): skirmishes on Assam–Meghalaya and India–Bangladesh borders; bandhs.
- Netherlands (S06, changed 9 Sep 2026): red Manipur; orange Mizoram, Nagaland, border areas with Myanmar, Bangladesh (Tripura, Assam, Meghalaya) and China (incl. Arunachal, Sikkim); strip width undefined.
- France Diplomatie (S05): page updated 23 Nov 2026; regional vigilance map not retrievable; no zoning seen. Recheck.
- Smartraveller (S07) and US State Dept (S08): only search summaries obtained (fetch timeout / HTTP 403): Manipur do-not-travel; US Level 2 overall, Northeast caution.
- Redesign: the original idea (Cherrapunji–Nongriat root bridges, Mawlynnong, Dawki/Shnongpdeng, Nagaland Khonoma/Dzükou, Arunachal Ziro/Tawang) was dropped because Dawki/Shnongpdeng/Mawlynnong sit at the Bangladesh border strip, Nagaland and Mizoram are Dutch orange, Arunachal needs PAP and is Dutch orange at the China border. The retained route stays about 25 km or more (estimate) north of the Bangladesh border.
- Status: **eligible**. Residual: insurgent incidents in upper Assam around national days, bandhs, inter-state boundary skirmishes.
