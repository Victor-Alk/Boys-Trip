# D56 — Chile — Granite Towers, Gravel Roads and Hanging Glaciers

Participant proposal of 2026-11-28 (second Chile route; complements D20 Atacama and D29 Argentine Patagonia). Research date: 2026-11-28. Prices marked "estimate" were not seen on a page.

## Overview

A 15-day route in Chilean Patagonia: the five-day W trek in Torres del Paine (Days 3–7), the Seno Otway penguin colony near Punta Arenas (Day 8), a flight to Aysén (Day 9) and a five-day self-drive on the northern Carretera Austral (Cerro Castillo, Puerto Río Tranquilo and the Marble Caves, Queulat and Puyuhuapi).

## Screening verdict

- **Seasonality:** good in both months; March is quieter than February but rainier and shorter-dayed.
- **Flights:** CDG–Santiago about €1,150 (estimate, copied from D20), plus four domestic segments (about €400, estimate).
- **Local costs:** high. Realistic total about €3,318 per person: outside budget (> €2,500), so not eligible for the final ranking.
- **Security:** eligible.
- **Cultural interest:** low to moderate; this is a landscape trip.
- **Adventure:** high; strenuous camped trek and gravel self-drive.
- **Independent-travel feasibility:** high, but the camps and the 4x4 must be booked far ahead.

## Both regions or one core?

Both fit in 15 days, but only because the connection is a flight and the Aysén half is cut to the northern loop. Torres del Paine is the core (five days, the one non-negotiable booking); Aysén is a five-day add-on. The ferry link was rejected: the Navimag Puerto Natales–Puerto Montt sailing takes about 4 days (S15), and it does not go to Aysén. A Puerto Yungay or Caleta Tortel extension would need another 4–5 days and is out of scope. The Punta Arenas–Balmaceda non-stop run by Aerovías DAP is seasonal (Nov–Feb) with about 4 flights a month (S16): it cannot be relied on for March, so the plan flies via Santiago with a same-day connection.

## Itinerary rationale

W trek west-to-east is common, but east-to-west puts the towers at sunrise on Day 4 and ends at the catamaran. The route stays on the Chilean side and does not repeat D29 (El Chaltén, Perito Moreno, Ushuaia) or D20 (Atacama). Aysén is limited to the north of Puerto Río Tranquilo to keep the longest drive under about 4.5 h. Flexible days: 2, 7, 9, 12.

## Transport and driving legality

- No car in the south: buses Punta Arenas–Natales–park and a catamaran (S08).
- 4x4 pickup or SUV from Balmaceda, same-place return (one-way rentals "prohibitively high", S17). About 90% of Puerto Montt–Puyuhuapi is paved; gravel and roadworks further south (S17). Two licensed drivers share the driving; add the second driver to the contract.
- IDP: rules not verified; carry one with the licences.
- Argentina is not entered; the cross-border insurance (10+ days ahead, S17) is irrelevant.

## Security assessment (checked 2026-11-28)

- France Diplomatie, country page updated 2 Nov 2026 (S01): no zone against travel; alerts for El Niño, enhanced vigilance, measles. Sécurité page dated 18 Nov 2026 (S02): only "vigilance renforcée" for Araucanía, Biobío, Los Lagos, Easter Island; zones last updated 5 Mar 2026. Aysén and Magallanes not named. Notes thefts and flat tyres on rental cars, frequent earthquakes.
- UK FCDO (S03–S05): no region warned against; change dated 9 Nov 2026. Armed muggings in Santiago and Valparaíso, severe wildfires, landmines near the southern border, tell authorities before hiking. Unrest and emergency in Araucanía and Biobío provinces.
- Netherlands (S06): yellow overall (changed 21 Aug 2026). Minefields in regions I, II, XV and XII (Magallanes); campfire ban.
- US State Department (S07): Level 2, from the D20 research (feed truncated today).
- Classification: **eligible**. The route avoids Araucanía, Biobío and Los Lagos and has no border crossing. Landmines near the Argentine border are the one route-adjacent hazard: stay on marked trails.

## Signature experiences

W trek in tents; sunrise at the Base de las Torres; Marble Caves boat; Queulat hanging glacier.

## Alternatives considered

- **Torres del Paine only (about 11 days, no car):** CDG–SCL–PUQ plus bus and camps; drops the Aysén flights, the car, fuel and Aysén accommodation (about €900 per person). Estimated realistic about €2,400, inside the exceptional band.
- **Aysén only:** SCL–BBA return, 8 days of car, more camping; estimated about €2,300–2,500.
- O circuit instead of the W: 7–9 days (S10), no time for Aysén.
- Futaleufú rafting and Caleta Tortel: omitted for distance and cost.
