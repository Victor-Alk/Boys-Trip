# D34 — Mongolia: Ice, Camels and Gers at -25 C

Researched 2026-11-28. Origin: participant proposal (2026-11-28). Source ids (S01-S19) refer to `data/destinations/D34-mongolia.json`.

## Overview

A 13-day winter expedition (indicative 1-13 March 2027): Ulaanbaatar, a flight to Mörön and Khatgal for the Khövsgöl Ice Festival, a flight to Dalanzadgad for Yolyn Am, Bayanzag and the Khongoryn Els dunes, then Hustai (Przewalski's horses) and Terelj. Self-drive is not feasible; a licensed driver-guide with a UAZ Furgon or Hyundai Starex van is used, with four domestic flights. Nights are in hotels or guesthouses (7) and heated family gers (3).

## Screening verdict

- **Seasonality:** Feb is the coldest month (Ulaanbaatar highs about -10 C, nights about -24 C; Khatgal colder), March about 8-10 C milder (UB highs about 0 C, Dalanzadgad about 4 C) (S18, commercial normals, indicative). Dry, sunny cold. Best month: March.
- **Flights:** CDG-Ulaanbaatar via Istanbul. Turkish Airlines fare page showed a return from EUR 923 for 27 Feb-5 Mar (S14, page date unclear). Realistic EUR 980 (representative), not a live quote.
- **Local costs:** about EUR 1,709 per person before contingency in the realistic scenario; cross-check: an 8-day winter Gobi/Terelj operator tour at USD 1,200 per person for four, full board (S11).
- **Security:** conditional (see below). No formal advice against travel.
- **Cultural interest:** high (herder families, Buddhism, shamanism, Ice Festival).
- **Adventure:** cold-driven; physically strenuous because of -20 to -30 C and remoteness, not technical.
- **Independent-travel feasibility:** low; the winter requires driver-guide, host families and domestic flights. Camping is not recommended.

## February vs March

- **February:** coldest. Tsagaan Sar eve 6 Feb, holiday 7-9 Feb 2027 (provisional, S17); family visits and closed shops; the Ice Festival (early March) is missed. Thousand Camel Festival in the Gobi around late Jan/early Feb (S12) also outside a mid-Feb window.
- **March:** better daylight (11-12 h), warmer afternoons, thickest lake ice, Ice Festival (2-3 March in 2026; 2027 dates not announced, S09). Preferred: March, 1-13 shiftable up to 5 days.

## Itinerary rationale

Khövsgöl is flown (UB-Mörön about 1 h 15 min) instead of a 10-12 h winter road drive (S09). The Gobi is flown to Dalanzadgad. Days 4-5 (festival) and 9 are flexible. The itinerary shifts as a block when the festival dates are published.

A request to include an Altai eagle-hunter "Spring Eagle Festival" in Bayan-Ölgii was investigated: no March festival was found; the main Golden Eagle Festival is in autumn (S19). Bayan-Ölgii also has a foot-and-mouth outbreak notice (S03). It is therefore excluded.

## Transport and driving legality

Only about 10% of roads outside Ulaanbaatar are paved, signs are minimal, accident rates are high, and a 1968 IDP is required (S02); the Netherlands says Dutch licences are only valid with an IDP (S04). Self-drive is not feasible. No motorcycle use. Operator vehicles: UAZ Furgon, Hyundai Starex or Mitsubishi Delica, speeds 30-65 km/h (S08). Domestic flights: Hunnu Air, Aero Mongolia, MIAT; routes are not all daily; 15 kg limit (S13). Winter schedules were not verified.

## Security assessment (checked 2026-11-28)

- **UK FCDO** (S01, S02): no against-all/all-but-essential warning; fuel-shortage warning including aviation fuel (updated 7 Aug 2026); extreme weather, high road accident rates, no network in much of the countryside.
- **France Diplomatie** (S03): page updated 14 Oct 2026; no country-wide formal warning found (colour not readable on the fetched text); bear attack near UB on 8 Oct 2026; foot-and-mouth in Bayan-Ölgii; fuel rationing 10 Sep 2026.
- **Netherlands Worldwide** (S04): yellow, updated 28 Aug 2026, shown valid until 28 Nov 2026 (renewal not yet seen).
- Smartraveller could not be fetched (timeout).
- **Classification: conditional**, because of the unresolved fuel-supply crisis, the Dutch advisory expiry date, and limited rural medical care in extreme cold. If fuel normalises and advisories remain unchanged it would be eligible. This is a security-methodology call; budget is separately outside the limit.

## Signature experiences

Ice Festival at Khatgal; a night with a camel-herding family at Khongoryn Els; frozen Yolyn Am and the Flaming Cliffs; Przewalski's horses at Hustai. Details and costs in the JSON.

## Alternatives considered

- **Cheaper variant:** drop the Gobi and keep Khövsgöl + Terelj/Hustai, with two flights fewer (about EUR 170 less) and about 4 fewer vehicle days (about EUR 95 per person); this reaches roughly EUR 2,500 realistic, but removes one of the trip's two pillars. Not built here.
- **Tsagaan Sar variant (February):** Ulaanbaatar and family visits around 7-9 Feb; trade-off: no Ice Festival.
- **Altai (Bayan-Ölgii)**: excluded, see above.
- **Trans-Mongolian rail:** slow, no added access.
