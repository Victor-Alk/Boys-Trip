# D55 — South Africa, Northeast: Panorama Route, Kruger, Zululand and the Drakensberg

Researched 2026-11-28. Participant-proposal (proposed 2026-11-28). Companion to D17 (Cape route); no overlap with it.

## Overview

A 15-day self-drive loop from Johannesburg (OR Tambo, JNB) through Gauteng, Mpumalanga and KwaZulu-Natal: Apartheid
Museum and Constitution Hill, the Panorama Route (Graskop, God's Window, Bourke's Luck Potholes, Three Rondavels),
two nights camping in Kruger National Park (Skukuza, Lower Sabie), Zululand (Pongola, Mkhuze, Hluhluwe-iMfolozi, the St Lucia
estuary and Cape Vidal), then the uKhahlamba-Drakensberg (Royal Natal, Tugela Falls, Giant's Castle San rock art)
and back to JNB. It is a loop, so there is no one-way fee. Eswatini and Sani Pass are optional extras, not in the
base plan or budget.

Total distance ≈ 3,200 km over 13 driving days (rental from day 2 to day 14).

## February vs March

- Temperatures (approximate normals, not a forecast): Johannesburg day 26–27 °C / night 15–16 °C; Kruger Lowveld day 31–33 °C / night 19–21 °C; St Lucia coast day 28–30 °C / night 21–22 °C; Drakensberg foothills day 24–27 °C / night 12–15 °C, with colder nights above 2,000 m.
- Rain: summer rainfall region. The Climate of South Africa article notes rain falls October to February "and is often heavy", with brief afternoon thunderstorms. Both months have storms; March is slightly drier and the mountains clearer. Cyclone remnants from the Mozambique Channel can bring heavy rain and flooding to KwaZulu-Natal and the Lowveld in February; France Diplomatie carried an El Niño vigilance alert on 29 Oct 2026 (a drier-summer signal for summer-rainfall regions, but the content of the alert was not readable).
- Kruger: green season, dense foliage, animals dispersed because water is everywhere, so sightings are harder than in the dry season; excellent birding, many young animals (impala lambing is late spring, so mostly calves by Feb), but heat and humidity.
- Malaria: the Kruger/Lowveld (Ehlanzeni district) and uMkhanyakude district in northern KwaZulu-Natal are malaria-risk areas; the national health department advises chemoprophylaxis September–May. Both months are in season. Hluhluwe-iMfolozi is at most low risk; St Lucia is on the margin; consult a travel clinic.
- Holidays: Ramadan 2027 (about 8 Feb – 9 Mar) and Lunar New Year (6 Feb) are not relevant. South African school terms resume early in the year (late-Jan start), so school-holiday crowds are absent. Human Rights Day is Sunday 21 March 2027 (Monday 22 March observed), a long-weekend road-traffic peak; the proposed window ends on Saturday 20 March, before it.
- Preferred month: March. Window 6–20 March 2027 (day 1 Sat 6 March evening departure; arrival JNB Sun 7 March; return flight leaves JNB the evening of Fri 19 March; arrival Paris Sat 20 March).

## Itinerary rationale

The base plan keeps three constraints: no driving after dark, mostly sealed roads, and a flexible day (day 10 Cape Vidal,
day 12 Tugela weather day, day 7 drive). Day 2 is the Apartheid Museum (open Tuesday to Sunday 09:00-17:00; arrival is
Sunday 7 March). Graskop is a two-night base for the Panorama Route. Kruger is entered at Phabeni Gate (not Numbi, see
Security), camping at Skukuza and Lower Sabie. Day 7 is the unavoidable long drive (about 6.5 h, 400 km) out of the park
via Crocodile Bridge, Malelane, Barberton and Piet Retief to Pongola, avoiding Eswatini (rental cross-border letters, R2,500
per border at one major renter, no additional drivers). Day 8 adds Mkhuze Game Reserve and Hluhluwe-iMfolozi, day 9 the
estuary cruise, day 10 Cape Vidal. Day 11 is the longest drive (520 km, about 7 h with a Ladysmith stop) to Royal Natal.
Day 12 is Tugela Falls, day 13 Giant's Castle rock art and day 14 a 5.5 h drive back to JNB with Constitution Hill on the
way before the evening flight (Friday 19 March, before the Human Rights Day long weekend).

## Transport and driving legality

- Drive on the left. Rental from JNB; pick-up on arrival day. Class: 7-seat SUV (Toyota Fortuner / Hilux double cab type) for four adults with luggage and camping kit; a 4x4 is not required for the base route (all roads are paved or graded gravel), except the optional Sani Pass, where South Africa only allows 4x4 vehicles on the pass.
- Licence: an English-language licence with photo and signature is generally accepted; FCDO notes a UK photocard is valid for 12 months; for a French licence, carry an International Driving Permit to be safe. Not verified against a rental company's terms today.
- Two of the four hold car licences; both must be named on the contract. Enterprise (South Africa) terms found: no additional drivers on rentals that include cross-border travel; minimum age 23.
- Cross-border (optional): Enterprise lets vehicles into Namibia, Botswana, Lesotho and Eswatini with 72 h notice, written authorisation and a R2,500 fee per border crossed. SARS has required an online customs declaration for vehicles on temporary import since 1 June 2026 (now including SACU-registered vehicles). A traveller declaration is compulsory for entering or leaving SA since 1 July 2026 (FCDO).
- One-way JNB to Durban: possible but a fee applies (Enterprise: amount only at booking); the loop avoids it. Durban is also not on the route's natural axis.
- Road safety: FCDO: fatal accidents common, especially around weekends and public holidays; keep the tank above half; potholes on some roads; do not cross protester roadblocks; criminals use spikes and fake police blue lights. France Diplomatie: do not stop for unmarked vehicles signalling, activate hazards and drive to a police station or fuel station. Avoid GPS shortcuts through secondary roads.
- Motorbikes: not used. Passengers stay in the car; guided tours and the Giant's Castle walks are on foot.

## Security assessment

Checked 2026-11-28 (France Diplomatie zone-map text dated 17 Mar 2026, page updated 4 Nov 2026).
- UK FCDO: no area of South Africa advised against; page last updated 5 Nov 2026 (fraud, taxis and e-hailing). Safety page: Kruger — SANParks advises against the Numbi Gate after a tourist was killed in a violent attack, and there have been protests and violence on the R538; two local tourists were killed in the far north of the park in May 2026 (a first of its kind in the park's history; suspects arrested), well away from the southern route; carjacking and smash-and-grab at junctions and fuel stations; fake police; OR Tambo robberies at gunpoint; protests can turn violent; load-shedding "significantly reduced" but can still occur.
- France Diplomatie: "vigilance renforcée" (yellow) for the whole country. Zones: Johannesburg (Melville, Hillbrow, Berea, Alexandra, Yeoville, old centre, Maboneng, bus station); Mpumalanga (Hazyview near Phabeni and Numbi: fake-police assaults, no night driving); KwaZulu-Natal only through Durban centre/seafront. The Drakensberg is not mentioned. Gauteng accounts for nearly half of the roughly 20,000 annual car hijackings.
- Nederland Wereldwijd: yellow (page updated 27 Nov 2026). Avoid driving after dark, avoid hitchhikers; no regional breakdown; malaria and rabies noted.
- US Department of State: Level 2 "Exercise increased caution" (crime, terrorism, unrest, kidnapping), issued 26 May 2025 (data feed, read today).
- Smartraveller (Australia): "exercise a high degree of caution"; confirmed only via a search summary, the page itself timed out.
- Verdict: **eligible**. No official source advises against any province on the route; conflict and terrorism are low; kidnapping risk (express kidnapping/ATM) is moderate. The residual risk is ordinary violent crime and road crime, which is higher in Gauteng and on KwaZulu-Natal highways than on D17's Western Cape route. Mitigations built into the plan: arrive in daytime with the rental car (no taxis, no ATMs at the airport), a guarded guesthouse in a safe suburb, Phabeni gate only, no night driving, locked doors, windows up, valuables out of sight, fuel above half.

## Signature experiences

1. Panorama Route and Graskop — God's Window (cliffs drop about 700 m), Bourke's Luck Potholes, Three Rondavels, Lisbon Falls.
2. Self-drive Kruger from rest-camp campsites — Sabie River road, Skukuza to Lower Sabie.
3. Hluhluwe-iMfolozi and St Lucia — rhino, oldest reserve (1895), hippo estuary cruise.
4. Tugela Falls — Amphitheatre rim via Sentinel car park and chain ladders (two sections of about 50 rungs); Tugela Gorge as the easier alternative.
5. Giant's Castle San rock art in the Main Caves.

## Alternatives considered

- Eswatini: Mlilwane/Hlane add a country but need cross-border letters, per-border fees and two extra border days; visa for French citizens not verified. Left out.
- Sani Pass (Lesotho): 4x4 only on the SA side, about 9 km, 1:3 gradients, border hours uncertain (06:00–18:00 vs 08:00–16:00 in two sources), weather closures, passport needed. Optional half-day from Underberg if the group hires a 4x4 or joins a guided 4x4 tour; not budgeted.
- One-way JNB to Durban: saves 520 km of backtracking but adds a drop-off fee and loses the Apartheid Museum/airport logic.
- Cheaper variant: skip Constitution Hill and Cape Vidal, camp at more nights, compact SUV.
