# D19 — Mexico: Oaxaca (Chiapas removed)

Research date: 2026-11-27. Verdict: **security eligible on a redesigned Oaxaca-only route**; realistic total about **€2,058 per person** (exceptional expenditure band); best month February.

## Overview
The original concept (Oaxaca plus Chiapas: San Cristóbal, Palenque, Yaxchilán/Bonampak, Agua Azul, Montebello, Sumidero) did not survive the advisory review. The delivered plan is 12 days in Oaxaca state: Oaxaca city, Monte Albán, southern craft/mezcal villages, three days on community trails in the Sierra Norte, a Mixteca convents day, one flexible day. Title: "Mexico — Zapotec Ridges and Mixtec Convents of Oaxaca".

## Screening verdict
- Seasonality: February dry and good; March hotter, crowds and prices rise towards Semana Santa.
- Flights: CDG–Mexico City–Oaxaca, estimate only (€1,000 realistic), no live quote.
- Local costs: about €945 per person realistic before contingency; see workings.
- Security: eligible after redesign, with open verification items.
- Cultural interest: high (Zapotec, Mixtec, food, convents). Adventure: moderate (three trek days at altitude).
- Independent travel: feasible; daylight driving only.

## February vs March
Oaxaca city (1,555 m): February mean high 31.1 / low 11.6 °C, 4.6 mm; March 33.3 / 13.6 °C, 17.6 mm (Wikipedia climate table, S08). Sierra Norte figures (nights 0–5 °C in Feb, 2–7 °C in Mar) are my estimates for 2,500–3,100 m, not station data. Carnival 2027: Shrove Tuesday 9 Feb (Easter 28 Mar). Benito Juárez holiday Mon 15 Mar. Spring equinox crowds ~20–21 Mar. Semana Santa 21–28 Mar 2027. Recommended: 15–26 Feb 2027. Ramadan and Lunar New Year not relevant.

## Itinerary rationale
Days 1 fly; 2–3 city and Monte Albán; 4 southern valleys by car; 5–7 Sierra Norte (Cuajimoloyas, Latuvi, Lachatao); 8 return; 9 Mixteca (Yanhuitlán, Teposcolula); 10 flexible; 11–12 fly home. Mitla and Hierve el Agua are deliberately an optional day-10 variant only, because they lie in the corridor of the federal road 190 towards Tehuantepec which France lists in orange (see below). Pacific coast dropped (Pacific highway carjackings per FCDO S04; extra flights/time). Village coordinates and trail stage lengths are approximate and unverified (the operator page published no routes or prices, S10).

## Transport and driving legality
Rental car days 4–9 only (7-seat vehicle, two licensed drivers, additional-driver clause to be checked). Compulsory Mexican third-party liability is bought locally (general knowledge, not verified on a rental site). IDP: not verified; typically a valid foreign licence is accepted. No motorbikes without licence; use taxis, colectivos, hired driver. No night driving (FCDO S04; NL S06 advises no travel after sunset in yellow areas). Beware of topes and community/teacher-union blockades; keep the day-10 buffer.

## Security assessment (checked 2026-11-27)
- **France Diplomatie** (S01, S02): country page updated 4 Nov 2026. The Sécurité page text (read through a page-summary tool; the map image has no alt text): Chiapas orange ("déconseillé sauf raison impérative") except Tuxtla Gutiérrez and San Cristóbal de las Casas; Guatemala border zone and Selva Lacandona orange; Oaxaca has no state-wide orange/red zone, only Oaxaca–Santo Domingo Tehuantepec (route 190) orange; rest yellow (vigilance renforcée). I could not confirm exact colours from the map itself.
- **UK FCDO** (S03, S04): page updated 24 Nov 2026. Nothing "against all travel". All-but-essential in Chiapas: 40 km of the Guatemalan border and Federal Highway 199 Rancho Nuevo–Chancalá junction (San Cristóbal–Palenque). Oaxaca not listed. FCDO also notes unofficial roadblocks, express kidnapping, safer first-class buses.
- **Netherlands** (S06): updated 26 Nov 2026. Oaxaca yellow; Chiapas yellow with orange for Route 199 and southern border (routes 307 and 190 except Tapachula); kidnapping of tourist minibuses near Ocosingo; high robbery risk; no travel after dark.
- **US State Department**: official page returned HTTP 403. Secondary report (S07) gives Oaxaca Level 2, nationwide advisory updated 29 May 2026; Chiapas level unconfirmed (other secondary hits suggested Level 3; unverified). Government-employee restrictions not read.
- **Smartraveller**: fetch timed out twice; not assessed. WebSearch budget was exhausted, limiting further verification.
Classification: Chiapas elements (Palenque via 199, Yaxchilán/Bonampak through Lacandon/border roads, Montebello within the border strip, Sumidero/Chiapa de Corzo outside France's two exceptions) would breach orange zones; removed. Oaxaca-only route avoids all orange zones found → **eligible**, with reassessment triggers listed in the JSON.
