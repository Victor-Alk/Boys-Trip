# D08 — Tunisia: Between Roman Ruins and the Sahara's Edge

Research notes, agent A08. Verification date: 2026-11-27. Route classified: **eligible** (redesigned route; see Security).

## 1. Overview

A 13-day clockwise loop: Tunis and Carthage, the underground Roman villas of Bulla Regia, Dougga, Kairouan, the amphitheatre of El Jem, the troglodyte pits of Matmata and the Amazigh hill villages of Tamezret and Toujane, a guided night in the Grand Erg Oriental dunes from Douz, the salt flat of Chott el Djerid and the Tozeur palm oasis, the ghorfa granaries of Medenine and finally Djerba. Open-jaw flights: CDG to Tunis, Djerba to CDG. A rented vehicle is used from day 3 to day 13; the desert night is run by a licensed Douz operator with its own 4x4.

The concept's identity (Roman, Amazigh, troglodyte, oasis) survives the security redesign. What was removed: Sbeitla, Chenini/Douiret/Ksar Ouled Soltane area, Ksar Ghilane, Remada and the south, the mountain oases of Chebika/Tamerza/Mides, and El Ghriba synagogue on Djerba (see section 7).

## 3. February vs March

- **February**: Ramadan starts about 8 Feb 2027, so most of the month falls inside it. Tunis/Carthage daytime 17 C (Tunis-Carthage normal 16.8 C max, 8.1 C min, 55 mm rain, 8 rain days). Tozeur 19.2 C max / 8.1 C min, 7 mm rain. Desert-camp nights are cold, near single digits and often lower on the dunes. Fewer tourists, cheaper.
- **March**: Tunis 19.4 C / 9.8 C, 46 mm rain; Tozeur 23 C / 11.6 C, 11 mm. Ramadan ends about 9 March, Eid al-Fitr about 9-10 March (moon-sighting). March has longer days and warm-but-not-hot desert days. Late-March spring bloom in the north.
- Recommendation: **March, after Eid (about 13-25 March 2027)**. This avoids Ramadan opening-hour changes and the Eid travel rush. If February is forced, expect shortened restaurant hours by day and a quieter pace; Ramadan is not a ban on travel but lunches need planning.
- Climate figures are Wikipedia-sourced 1991-2020 station normals (Tunis-Carthage and Tozeur). Douz and Matmata have no station figures here; they should sit close to Tozeur/Gabes values but this is not verified.

## 5. Transport and driving legality

- **Licence**: FCDO says a 1968 international driving permit is valid for up to one year in Tunisia, with a green card for insurance; check which convention applies to the licence country. Two travellers hold car licences; the others do not drive. No motorcycle or quad riding by anyone without the proper licence; the desert component is as a passenger in a 4x4 or on a camel.
- **Rental**: international and local agencies at Tunis-Carthage, Djerba and Tozeur airports. One-way (Tunis to Djerba) fee estimated EUR 60-150; not quoted. A compact SUV or minivan fits four adults and gear. 4x4 rental is not needed on tarmac; desert tracks are done with a guide and the operator's vehicle.
- **Roads**: A1 Tunis-Sousse motorway, then main roads; the Chott el Djerid causeway is paved. Hazards (FCDO): potholes after rain, unannounced speed bumps, sand drifts, unlit roads and poor lane discipline. Avoid night driving.
- **Checkpoints**: approach slowly, carry passports and licence (FCDO).
- **Guide for desert**: not verified in an official text. In the southern buffer zone a governor's authorisation is required (see section 7); the plan keeps to the Douz edge of the erg through a licensed operator who handles any required paperwork.

## 7. Security assessment

Checked 2026-11-27 (France Diplomatie, FCDO, Nederland Wereldwijd fetched; US State via search snippet only because travel.state.gov returned HTTP 403).

- **France Diplomatie** (page updated 15 Sept 2026; map 13 Mar 2026): red = extreme-south Saharan military zone near the Libyan/Algerian borders (prior authorisation), and the centre-west mountains (Orbata, Selloum, Semema, Chambi, Mghila); orange = desert south and east of the line Bir Rijm Maatoug - Borj Bourguiba - Ben Guerdane, the Chambi/Semema/Orbata massifs' surroundings, rural zone between Kasserine, Sbeitla, Sidi Bouzid and Gafsa (roads Gafsa-Kasserine and Gafsa-Sidi Bouzid usable), and border strip within 5 km in Jendouba/Le Kef; yellow = rest of country, with Table de Jugurtha only on organised tours. Tataouine, Remada and Ksar Ghilane are not named.
- **FCDO** (updated 24 July 2026): against all travel: Chaambi NP, military zones Salloum, Sammamma, Mghila; the militarised zone south of El Borma and Dhehiba; within 20 km of the Libyan border north of Dhehiba; Ben Guerdane. Against all but essential: Kasserine Governorate including Sbeitla; areas north and west of Ghardimaou; within 20 km of the Algerian border in El Kef and Jendouba south of Jendouba town; within 10 km of the rest of the Algerian border south of Kasserine; within 75 km of the Libyan border including Remada and El Borma (excluding Zarzis, the C118 and Medenine Governorate north of that road). Terrorism threat "likely"; state of emergency since 2015; 2023 shooting near El Ghriba on Djerba.
- **Nederland Wereldwijd** (changed 17 Sept 2026): most of the country yellow; red for the desert south of the Al Borma-Remada-Ben Gardane line, the Libyan and Algerian border zones and the Chaambi mountains; orange for the area south of Tataouine and north of that line and for Kasserine and surroundings. Ksar Ghilane not named.
- **US State Department**: Level 2 (issued 21 May 2026 per search snippet), with Do Not Travel areas near borders and Chaambi (not confirmed from the official page).
- **Southern buffer zone**: a presidential decree (reported as n 2026-206 of 12 Aug 2026) extends the military buffer zone for a year; entry for tourism needs the governor's authorisation. Not verified on an official page.

**Route design**: stay on the Gabes-Medenine-Djerba side; skip Tataouine and everything south of it; skip Kasserine (Sbeitla); skip Mides, Tamerza and Chebika (Chebika is about 8 km from the Algerian border, inside the FCDO 10 km strip); keep Bulla Regia (north of Jendouba town, east of Ghardimaou, outside the FCDO wording, but near enough to be re-verified); skip El Ghriba. Douz is in Kebili Governorate and is not named in any advisory; desert camping stays on the Douz edge of the erg, not at Ksar Ghilane.

**Classification: eligible**, with reassessment triggers. Residual issues: Dutch orange wording is unclear about the exact extent south of Tataouine (avoided); Bulla Regia distance from border zones.
