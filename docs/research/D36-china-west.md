# D36 — China West: Sichuan's Tibetan Highlands to Dunhuang

Participant proposal (28 November 2026). Research date 2026-11-28. Data: `data/destinations/D36-china-west.json`.

## Overview
A 15-day route (17–31 March 2027): Chengdu (pandas, hot pot), Kangding, Tagong (3,700 m) and Danba in Garzê, a flight to Xining (Kumbum Monastery), Tongren, Xiahe (Labrang Monastery), then Lanzhou by car and high-speed trains through Zhangye (Danxia), Jiayuguan (fort) and Dunhuang (Mogao Caves, Mingsha dunes), and out via Beijing. It lies north of D03 (Yunnan) and does not overlap with it. No self-drive (foreign licences are invalid); hired van with driver on road days.

## Screening verdict
- Seasonality: March good, February poor (Spring Festival 5–13 Feb, Losar about 7 Feb, Dunhuang nights about -10 °C) [S11, S14, S26].
- Flights: estimate only. EUR 780 realistic open-jaw CDG–TFU / PEK–CDG, anchored on an aggregator "from EUR 522" return [S24].
- Local costs: realistic EUR 1,334 local + EUR 160 contingency; headline EUR 2,274 per person (Exceptional expenditure).
- Security: **conditional** (see below).
- Cultural interest: very high; adventure: moderate; independent-travel feasibility: limited (hired drivers, no camping).

## February vs March
Spring Festival is 5–13 February 2027 (State Council notice) [S11]. Trains, flights and hotels are crowded or shut, and Losar is about 7 February [S26]; France Diplomatie warns that Ganzi, Aba and Litang may be restricted around Tibetan New Year (February–March) and certain anniversaries [S01]. Labrang's Monlam falls in the first Tibetan month (about mid to late February); I could not find 2027 dates and did not confirm that foreigners are admitted to the busiest days. Dunhuang is about -10 to 5 °C in February [S14]. March: 0–16 °C at Dunhuang, windy; Xining 10 / -4 °C [S12]. The window 17–31 March starts after the 10, 14 and 16 March anniversaries; the Ganzi days are 19–22 March. Ramadan ends about 9 March; no impact.

Feb alternative (if the group wants Monlam): 13–27 February, accepting Ganzi closure risk, cold and the post-holiday return rush. Not recommended.

## Itinerary rationale
Altitude first, in steps: Chengdu 500 m, Kangding 2,560 m, Tagong 3,700 m, down to Danba 1,900 m, then Xining 2,275 m and Xiahe 2,900 m. Rail corridor last. Flexible Day 6 (Danba–Chengdu, can absorb a closed Ganzi road) and Day 14 (Dunhuang–Beijing, absorbs a missed Mogao slot or sandstorm). Day 14 ends with a night near Beijing airport and a morning flight home.

Left out, with reasons:
- **Tibet Autonomous Region:** needs a permit and an organised tour; permits can be stopped without notice [S05, S07]; floods in Gyirong in August 2026 [S02, S03]. Not feasible for an independent group.
- **Xinjiang:** France yellow; FCDO describes arbitrary detentions and phone checks of foreigners [S01, S05, S07]. Excluded.
- **Larung Gar (Sertar):** closed to foreigners in practice, no published notice [S19, S18].
- **Jiuzhaigou and Huanglong:** in Aba prefecture (access restrictions around anniversaries, as with Ganzi), 8 h by road or a winter-delay-prone flight, frozen lakes in winter [S15, S20]. Dropping them also saves two days.
- **Qinghai Lake:** touches Haiyan and Tianjun, which France lists as closed to foreigners [S01]; semi-frozen in March.

## Transport and driving legality
Foreign licences and IDPs are not valid; a temporary permit takes 1–5 business days with certified translation [S18, S04]. All road legs use a hired van with driver (CNY 800–1,200 a day) [S18]. Trains: Lanzhou West–Zhangye West (about 18 pairs a day, CNY 113–171) [S17]; Jiayuguan South–Dunhuang (D2749 16:57–19:26, CNY 109, about 2.5 h) [S16]. Flights Chengdu–Xining and Dunhuang–Beijing: not quoted. No motorcycles.

## Security assessment (checked 2026-11-28)
- France Diplomatie Sécurité (page dated 15 Sep 2026, zones updated 5 Mar 2026) [S01]: whole of China green except Xinjiang yellow. Sichuan: access to Ganzi, Aba, Litang may be restricted around Tibetan New Year and anniversaries. Qinghai: a list of districts closed to foreigners, including Xining city, Huangzhong (only Kumbum open), Haiyan, Tianjun, Menyuan, Delingha and Guoluo counties. This list is not consistent with current practice (hotels and tour operators host foreigners in Xining) and I did not find a newer official statement. The alerts page (updated 16 Nov 2026) has only Tibet floods and typhoons [S02].
- UK FCDO (updated 27 Aug 2026) [S03–S06]: no region of Sichuan, Gansu or Qinghai marked "against all travel"; TAPs in neighbouring provinces may be restricted even with a permit; detention and exit-ban warnings.
- Nederland Wereldwijd: yellow for China (changed 28 Aug 2026) [S07].
- US State Department: Level 2, updated 18 Oct 2026 (data feed) [S08].
- Smartraveller: page timed out; not read.
Classification: **conditional** (unresolved access restrictions in Tibetan prefectures and Qinghai, plus the visa-free end date). Not excluded: no official "against all travel" for the route.

## Signature experiences
Kham highlands (Tagong–Danba); Labrang and its kora; Mogao and Mingsha; Zhangye Danxia and Jiayuguan. See JSON for costs and organisation.

## Alternatives considered
Cheaper: drop the Dunhuang–Beijing flight and finish by train (Dunhuang–Lanzhou–Xi'an) with a different return; skip Tongren. Safer: skip Ganzi and add Leshan/Dujiangyan and Siguniang from Chengdu. Winter: the whole trip in March only.
