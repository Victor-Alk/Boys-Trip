# D47 — Namibia: Dunes, Fog Coast and Etosha by Rooftop Tent

Researched 2026-11-28. Origin: participant proposal (28 Nov 2026). Security: eligible. Realistic total about EUR 3,075 per person (outside budget, informational only).

## Overview
A 14-day self-drive loop (vehicle hired 12 days, about 2,900 km) from Windhoek: Sesriem and Sossusvlei, Walvis Bay and Swakopmund, Cape Cross, Spitzkoppe, Twyfelfontein in Damaraland, Etosha (Okaukuejo, Halali, Namutoni) and back via the B1. Nine camping nights, two guesthouse nights in Swakopmund, one 4x4 with two rooftop tents. Proposed window 6–19 March 2027.

## Screening verdict
- Seasonality: green season (Jan–Mar). Hot in the desert (about 35 C), rainy in the north, cool foggy coast. March is preferable to February.
- Flights: no nonstop; Lufthansa-group one-stop fares seen as statistics at about EUR 776–826 for Feb 2027 (S14). March 2027 fares were not on sale when checked.
- Local costs: dominated by the 4x4 hire (EUR 182/day plus EUR 28/day reduced excess).
- Security: eligible (below).
- Cultural interest: moderate; rock art (Twyfelfontein, Spitzkoppe), colonial history; Himba visit not scheduled.
- Adventure: strong, mostly driving, heat and dunes.
- Independent travel: standard self-drive country; only two of four can drive.

## February vs March
February is the wettest, hottest month: floods in northern riverbeds, dispersed animals in Etosha, but lowest rental rates and newborn animals. March has tapering rain, slightly cooler nights and improving viewing (S15: sightings improve towards April). Temperatures are typical values from secondary climate sites, not official normals (Namibia Meteorological Service normals were not found). Preferred: March, ending before Independence Day (21 March) and Easter (28 March).

## Itinerary rationale
Sossusvlei is done early before fatigue. The coast gives a cool break (17–24 C in Feb); Sandwich Harbour is done with a local operator so the group does not drive the tide-dependent beach. Spitzkoppe and Twyfelfontein give community-run camping and rock art. Etosha comes last with three waterhole camps. Honest judgement: in the green season animals disperse and viewing is harder than in the dry season (S15), but the park is quiet and lush and permanent waterholes still work; if guaranteed game viewing matters, travel in the dry season instead. Flexible days: day 6, day 9 and a buffer on day 12.

Left out: Fish River Canyon and Lüderitz/Kolmanskop (about 1,500 km more). The multi-day Fish River hike is a May–September product (most sources say 1 May–15 Sept; S11), so it is closed in Feb–March; viewpoints stay open but are very hot (not verified). Himba: not scheduled; if added, choose a community-run visit near Opuwo and ask how revenue is shared, rather than dropping in on a village. This was not researched in detail (search found nothing specific).

## Transport and driving legality
Left-hand traffic. Rental from Windhoek (S07, S08). Two licence holders share driving; the contract must list both. IDP requirement not verified. Gravel limit officially 100 km/h but French advice is 80 km/h maximum (S01, S02); many rental policies stop covering above 80. Night driving strongly discouraged. Rollovers and tyre blowouts on gravel are the main tourist risk.

## Security assessment (checked 2026-11-28)
- France Diplomatie (S01): detailed advice, no regional ban in the text; crime in Windhoek, strong advice against night driving, 80 km/h on gravel. The security-map colour is an image and could not be read; the 10 Sept 2026 general "vigilance renforcée" alert does not visibly name Namibia.
- UK FCDO (S02, S04, S06): no regions listed against travel; page updated 10 Dec 2025.
- Netherlands (S03): yellow, updated 1 Oct 2026; flooding in northern Namibia Jan–April.
- Smartraveller (timed out) and US State Department (HTTP 403): not retrieved.
Classification: eligible. No conflict, terrorism or kidnapping concern found; ordinary risks are roads, urban crime, wildlife, floods and heat.

## Entry
French citizens need a visa since 1 April 2025, on arrival at Windhoek or Walvis Bay or by e-visa; NAD 1,600 (S04); passport valid 6 months, 3 blank pages (S05). One source mentions an extra NAD 400 fee (unconfirmed). Stay length differs between sources (30 vs 90 days).

## Signature experiences
Dawn at Dune 45 and Deadvlei; Sandwich Harbour; Spitzkoppe camping; Twyfelfontein engravings and desert elephants; Etosha waterhole camps.
