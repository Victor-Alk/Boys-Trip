# D50 — Morocco: Granite, Saffron and the Saghro, Beyond the Classic Circuit

Researched 2026-11-28. Later addition (participant proposal). Estimates are labelled; nothing here is a live quote.

## Overview
A 13-day route in southern Morocco built to avoid the Marrakech, Fès, Merzouga and Aït Ben Haddou circuit: Agadir, Legzira and Sidi Ifni on the Atlantic, Tafraoute and the Ameln valley (two tent nights), Taliouine and the Jbel Siroua, then a four-day mule-supported trek across the Jbel Saghro from Boumalne Dadès, ending with a transit night in Marrakech. About 1,450 km by rented SUV with two drivers. Marrakech is a transit night only.

## Screening verdict
- Seasonality: March good, February fair (Ramadan, freezing nights).
- Flights: direct, low-cost (March 2027 Paris–Agadir returns about EUR 99–125 in fare-tracker snapshots).
- Local costs: low; the trek is the largest item.
- Security: eligible on reviewed sources (see below) with caveats.
- Cultural interest: Amazigh villages, saffron and argan cooperatives, ksour.
- Adventure: guided four-day trek, rated demanding (3/5).
- Independent feasibility: self-drive on paved roads, guided trek.

## February vs March
February: Tafraoute almond blossom (festival usually in February; a 2024 edition on 23–24 Feb), but Ramadan (about 8 Feb to 9 Mar 2027, moon-sighting dependent) affects trek crews, small-town restaurants and opening hours, and Saghro nights go below -5 °C at 2,000 m. March (13–25 March recommended) follows Eid al-Fitr (about 9–10 March). Blossom is over; days 16–23 °C, nights 2–9 °C in the Anti-Atlas and about -3 to 4 °C on the Saghro plateau. Temperatures are planning ranges, not verified station normals. A February trip is possible with adjustments (self-catering, rest buffer, fasting crew) but judged less reliable.

## Itinerary rationale
Day 1 fly to Agadir, Tiznit. Day 2 Legzira and Sidi Ifni. Day 3 to Tafraoute (tent night 1). Day 4 Ameln valley, Aït Mansour gorges, Jbel Lekst foothills (tent night 2). Day 5 to Taliouine. Day 6 Siroua day walk (flexible). Day 7 to Boumalne Dadès (about 6 h). Days 8–11 Saghro trek, three bivouacs, finish at Boumalne where the car waits. Day 12 over the Tizi n'Tichka to Marrakech. Day 13 fly home.
Omitted on purpose: M'hamid/Erg Chigaga/Lake Iriki and Tata–Akka (too close to the Algerian border zone; Netherlands orange; France map not confirmed), Plage Blanche (residual mines in south-east Guelmim per France), Toubkal winter ascent (needs crampons and a guide; kept as a possible alternative), the Rif.

## Transport and driving legality
Rental SUV from Agadir, dropped at Marrakech (one-way fee). Two licence holders. FCDO advises a 1968 IDP and green card for UK drivers; French licences are normally accepted, confirm with the rental firm. No piste driving (rental contracts usually forbid it); no motorcycles, scooters or quads without the licence. The Tizi n'Tichka (2,260 m) can close in snow.

## Security assessment (checked 2026-11-28)
- Netherlands Worldwide (changed 30-07-2026): yellow for the rest of Morocco; orange for the Algerian border area (military zone, land border closed since 1994); red for the Mauritanian border area of Western Sahara (landmines).
- UK FCDO (updated 19 Aug 2026): regional-risks page names only the closed Algerian border (do not try to cross) and Western Sahara as disputed; terrorism attacks 'likely'; road accidents frequent; hire a guide for Atlas hikes.
- France Diplomatie: country page updated 10 Sept 2026 with a regional vigilance alert; the detailed zones page returned 404/unreadable, so zones were taken from the April 2026 PDF fiche as summarised by a search result (Mauritanian border formally advised against; residual mines in south-east Guelmim province). The Algerian border wording on the French map is unconfirmed.
- US State Department: page returned HTTP 403; not read. Smartraveller: timed out.
Route is more than about 200 km from the Algerian border and outside all named zones. Status: eligible, with the above gaps flagged for reassessment.

## Signature experiences
Four days across the Saghro with mules (about EUR 150 pp); Legzira's sea arch at low tide; two tent nights at Tafraoute; Taliouine saffron and a Siroua day walk.

## Alternatives considered
Draa valley to Erg Chigaga/Lake Iriki (wilder than Merzouga, but in the Algerian border area; dropped); Tata and Akka oases with rock art (same concern, plus distance); Toubkal winter ascent (a guide with crampons; stays optional); the Rif (Talassemtane, Akchour; a different, greener trip, not researched in depth). A cheaper variant: skip the Siroua and cut Taliouine, saving about a day of driving.

## Open questions and reassessment triggers
Read France's interactive map and the US advisory directly; outfitter quote and equipment supplied; snow on the Tichka, Siroua and Saghro passes; exact Eid date; rental contract terms on one-way drop and additional drivers.
