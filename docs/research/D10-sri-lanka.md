# D10 — Sri Lanka: Sacred Cities, Leopard Lakes and the Tea Escarpment

Research date: 2026-11-27. Planning figures are estimates unless a source is cited; sources S01–S30 are listed in the JSON file and summarised at the end.

## Overview
A 13-day loop (2 international travel days) from Colombo through Wilpattu, the Cultural Triangle (Anuradhapura, Mihintale, Ritigala, Sigiriya, Dambulla, Polonnaruwa), Kandy, the tea hills (Nuwara Eliya, Ella, Haputale) and Udawalawe, returning via Negombo. Recommended window 8–20 February 2027. About 1,000 km of road over 11 land days, with a van and driver.

## Screening verdict
- **Seasonality:** February is the strong month (dry zone and hill country dry, cooler). March is hotter, with thunderstorms from mid-March. The Minneriya/Kaudulla elephant gathering is a July–October event (S20), so it is NOT part of this plan; Udawalawe provides year-round elephants, and Gal Oya boat safaris are best about March–July (S21).
- **Flights:** CDG–CMB fare tables seen today: Turkish from about EUR 718, SriLankan from about EUR 884 (S14, S15). The 2026 Middle East conflict disrupted Gulf hubs and cut arrivals (S16); the non-stop or Istanbul routings avoid them.
- **Local costs:** low; realistic local about EUR 1,080 pp incl. contingency.
- **Security:** eligible (below).
- **Cultural interest:** very high; wildlife strong; hiking moderate.
- **Independent feasibility:** good with a driver; self-drive feasible but not recommended; tuk-tuk self-drive legally unresolved.

## February vs March
| | February | March |

| Dry zone | dry, about 31–32 C day, 22 C night | 33–34 C, higher humidity, storms from mid-month |
| Hill country | driest, clearer views, 8–10 C nights in Nuwara Eliya | more afternoon rain and cloud |

Climate numbers are climatological planning ranges (S27, S28), not forecasts. Ramadan has minimal effect (Buddhist majority). Lunar New Year is not relevant.

## Itinerary rationale
Wilpattu is quieter than Yala and a camp night gives a wild opening. Cultural days are paced with bicycles, and Day 6 at Sigiriya is flexible. Day 7 Polonnaruwa–Kandy is the longest drive (about 4–4.5 h plus site time). Tea country uses the Nanu Oya–Ella railway, reopened 20 June 2026 (S12); Colombo–Kandy and Kandy–Nanu Oya restoration was still reported in September 2026 (S13), so Kandy to Nuwara Eliya is by road. Udawalawe replaces the east and north, which would add 6+ h legs. Day 12 is a second buffer before the return flight.

## Transport and driving legality
- **Self-drive car:** requires a valid full licence, a physical 1949 IDP and a Sri Lankan recognition permit (AA Ceylon; about USD 40 per rental guides) or a DMT temporary licence available at Colombo airport since Aug 2025 (S04, S09). Only the two licensed travellers could drive. France recommends hiring a car with driver and avoiding night driving (S01); FCDO notes frequent accidents (S04).
- **Tuk-tuk self-drive (e.g. Tuk Tuk Rental):** needs a licence class covering three-wheelers plus a local permit. DMT (May 2025) said a local test and training are needed and the airport licence excludes tuk-tuks (S09, S10). Police HQ said in Nov 2025 that IDP holders may not drive three-wheelers; a Court of Appeal interim order (Feb 2026) restored the previous status quo and no final ruling was found (S08). The two unlicensed travellers cannot drive tuk-tuks in any case. Verdict: not in the plan; ride tuk-tuks with local drivers only.
- **Hired driver-guide (selected):** also bypasses fuel quotas (QR system since 15 Mar 2026 with tourism allowance; current status unverified, S11).
- **Trains/buses:** train for Nanu Oya–Ella (reserved fares about LKR 440–6,000 by class, S22); buses are fast and crowded (S04).

## Security assessment (checked 2026-11-27)
- France Diplomatie: whole country "vigilance renforcée"; no red/orange zone; further attacks cannot be excluded; unexploded mines in north and east; Cyclone Arnab and El Niño alerts (S01, S02).
- UK FCDO: no against-travel advice; fuel cap, flight disruption, flood/landslide warnings; terrorism cannot be ruled out; northern and eastern mine warnings (S03–S05).
- Smartraveller: "exercise a high degree of caution" (listing dated 14 Aug 2026; page fetch timed out) (S06). US State: Level 2 (14 Oct 2025; seen via search summary, page returned 403) (S07). Netherlands Worldwide could not be fetched (404).
- Classification: **eligible**. Conflict low, terrorism moderate, kidnapping low. The 2022 unrest is the reference for protest disruption.

## Signature experiences
Wilpattu night camp and dawn safari; cycling the sacred cities; Mihintale sunrise; train and Ella Rock; Udawalawe elephants. Temples need covered shoulders and knees; visit tea-estate communities through local hosts rather than as attractions.

## Alternatives considered
- **Jaffna and the Tamil north:** Nallur Kovil, Delft, Keerimalai (S30); adds two long days and mined-area precautions (S05); better as a separate trip.
- **East coast (Trincomalee, Arugam Bay, Gal Oya):** February transitional; Gal Oya best from March; too far for this loop.
- **Less-visited kingdoms:** Yapahuwa (north-west) and Mulkirigala (south) can fill Day 12; Ritigala access rules are uncertain (S29).
- **Minneriya gathering:** July–October only.
- **Safer/cheaper:** keep the van with driver throughout and skip camping.
