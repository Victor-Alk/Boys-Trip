# D38 — Indonesia — Sulawesi: Cliff Graves, Cave Art and Tarsiers

Researched 2026-11-28 from a participant proposal. Security status: **conditional**. Realistic total per person: about **€2,139** (flights included), "Exceptional expenditure". Preferred window: **13–27 March 2027**.

## Overview
A 15-day route (13 days on the ground) at the two ends of Sulawesi, entirely off the Bali and Java circuit. South: Makassar (Fort Rotterdam, Paotere schooner harbour), Maros-Pangkep karst (Rammang-Rammang longboat, Leang-Leang open cave-art sites), Toraja country around Rantepao (Kete Kesu, Lemo, Londa, Batutumonga walk), and the Tana Beru/Bira pinisi yards. A flight north to Manado leads to Tomohon, Tangkoko, Lembeh Strait and Bunaken. **Central Sulawesi (Poso, Palu, Togean, Lore Lindu, Bada) was left out** on security grounds.

## Screening verdict
- Seasonality: poor in February, fair in March. Both are wet season.
- Flights: estimate €1,100 realistic, open-jaw CDG–UPG / MDC–CDG. No live quote was seen.
- Local costs: low, about €930 per person incl. contingency €111 for 13 days (estimates, not quotes).
- Security: conditional (see below).
- Cultural interest: very high (cave art, Toraja funerary culture, pinisi).
- Adventure: moderate (one 3–4 h walk, snorkelling).
- Independent travel: possible, but a hired driver is the practical and safer norm.

## February vs March
Makassar normals (1991–2020): February 563 mm, 26 rainy days, 31.2/24.9 C; March 391 mm, 23 rainy days, 31.7/25.1 C. Manado (1961–1990): February 361 mm, March 338 mm, with no real dry season. Toraja highland values are our estimate. North-coast seas are said to be roughest December–February. Ramadan (about 8 Feb–9 Mar) closes daytime eateries in the Muslim-majority south (Makassar, Pare-Pare, Bira); Toraja and Minahasa are Christian-majority. Eid al-Fitr about 9–10 March brings mass domestic travel and high fares. Therefore: depart 13 March. Easter is 28 March; Good Friday 26 March falls after the Toraja days.

## Itinerary rationale
Day 1 depart; 2 Makassar; 3 Maros karst and Leang-Leang, night Pare-Pare; 4 Rantepao and Kete Kesu; 5 Lemo and Londa; 6 Batutumonga walk (flexible); 7 return to Makassar (8–9 h); 8 Bira; 9 Tana Beru yards and back to Makassar (flexible); 10 flight to Manado, Tomohon; 11 Tangkoko; 12 tarsiers, Lembeh (flexible); 13 Bunaken; 14 depart; 15 arrive Paris. Heavy driving days: 4, 7, 8, 9. The Rantepao–Makassar day could be split with a night in Pare-Pare.

**What visitors can actually see at the Maros cave art:** open sites at Leang-Leang (hand stencils, painted walls) are visitable with a local guide. The best-known dated panels are reported not to be open to ordinary visitors; this was not verified with the authorities and should be confirmed. **Toraja funerals:** Rambu Solo' peaks July–October, with ceremonies in other months. Do not plan around one; if invited, attend as a quiet guest in dark clothing with a small gift. The Toraja airport (TRT) route from Makassar is described as seasonal (June–October, Wings Air) so the road is planned.

## Transport and driving legality
Car with driver throughout. Self-drive is not advised: left-hand traffic, motorbikes, livestock, landslides. A 1949-convention IDP is required. Nobody may ride a scooter or motorbike without a licence; legal alternatives are a car with driver, ojek as passenger where licensed, or walking.

## Security assessment (checked 2026-11-28)
- France Diplomatie, security page updated 15 Sept 2026: Papua advised against except for imperative reasons; piracy listed in the Banda, Java and Celebes seas under the "déconseillées sauf raison impérative" heading; Poso-area terrorists target police; sea transport dangerous; high quake/tsunami/volcano risk; flood and landslide risk in the rainy season.
- UK FCDO, updated 18 Sept 2026: no region named as against-travel; Central Sulawesi unsettled; piracy risk in the Sulu and Celebes seas; terror threat high; vessels poorly maintained.
- Nederland Wereldwijd (changed 20 Oct 2026): orange for Papua only; yellow elsewhere; high alert on Lokon and Karangetang.
- Smartraveller and the US State Department could not be fetched (timeout and HTTP 403).

Reasoning: the land route is clear. The French piracy wording covers entire sea names, so short coastal boat trips at Bunaken (Celebes Sea) are in an ambiguous zone. I did not exclude the route, but classified it conditional and asked for a check with the embassy or insurer. Lokon (Tomohon) summit is not climbed.

## Signature experiences
