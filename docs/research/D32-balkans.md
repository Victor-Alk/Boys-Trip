# D32 — Balkans: Stone Towns, Ancient Lakes and the Sarajevo Siege Tunnel

Participant proposal (2026-11-28). Researched 2026-11-28. Route: Albania, North Macedonia, Montenegro, Bosnia and Herzegovina.

## Overview
A 15-day one-way self-drive loop Tirana to Sarajevo: Berat, Benjë hot springs, Gjirokastër, Butrint/Sarandë, Korçë, Ohrid, Shkodër, Kotor, Durmitor/Žabljak and the Tara bridge, Sarajevo (siege sites, Tunnel Museum), Mostar and Blagaj. About 1,900 km, no day above about 5 h of driving.

## Screening verdict
- **Seasonality:** March (13–27 March 2027) preferred; February overlaps Ramadan and has more snow.
- **Flights:** estimate EUR 190 / 280 / 380 pp, open-jaw. Low-cost direct fares exist from Paris Beauvais (BVA), not CDG; Sarajevo return leg unverified.
- **Local costs:** low (guesthouse twin about EUR 48, food about EUR 26/day).
- **Security:** eligible, no area advised against; landmines, Tirana protests and road safety are the material points.
- **Independent-travel feasibility:** good, with one large caveat — rental firms often refuse Bosnia and one-way international drops.
- **Cultural interest:** high. **Adventure:** modest, winter-limited.

## February vs March
February: Ramadan (about 8 Feb to 9 Mar, moon-sighting dependent), snow likely on the Ersekë road, Durmitor and Sarajevo, some sites closed, cheapest. March: after Eid (about 10 March), milder, still wet in the south, snow mostly confined to Durmitor. Accursed Mountains/Theth are largely snowbound in both months and are not on the route. Window ends before Western Easter (28 March).

## Itinerary rationale
South-to-north keeps the longest drive (Ohrid–Shkodër, about 5 h) mid-trip and puts the Durmitor day (weather-dependent) before the Bosnia leg. Contingency days: 7 (Ohrid) and 11 (Durmitor). Mostar is placed last so the final airport drive is 2 h 15 min. Mavrovo was considered and dropped to avoid a longer day. Albanian Riviera coastal road (Llogara pass) dropped because it adds snow risk and a detour.

## Transport and driving legality
Two licence-holders share the car. Written permission from the rental firm and a Green Card are needed for Montenegro, North Macedonia and Bosnia (S14, via search summary; unverified in detail). IDP requirement depends on licence country. Winter: chains for Montenegrin mountain roads (S19); satnav may suggest the closed P14 Plužine–Prevoj Sedlo road. Albanian roads: dangerous, unlit, drive by day (S09).

## Security assessment (checked 2026-11-28)
- France Diplomatie Albania (23 Oct 2026): alert on demonstrations and violence in Tirana since 13 Oct; avoid Ministry of Education area, Durrës road, Skanderbeg Square surroundings. Map colour not readable with the tool. Bosnia page (10 Sep 2026): no specific warning visible; map colour unreadable.
- UK FCDO: Albania (15 Oct), Bosnia (13 Aug), Montenegro (13 Aug), North Macedonia (13 Aug): no area advised against; Bosnia landmines remain, stay on roads and paved areas, attacks cannot be ruled out.
- Nederland Wereldwijd: green for all four; Bosnia landmines, wild camping prohibited; Albania landmines near Kosovo border; Montenegro avalanche and road risks.
- Kosovo/northern Serbia are off route. Classification: eligible.

## Signature experiences
Benjë hot springs; Sarajevo siege walk and Tunnel Museum (about 840 m tunnel built March–June 1993, S12); Durmitor winter day with the Tara bridge; the four-country religious-heritage road.

## Alternatives considered
Cheaper/safer: Albania plus North Macedonia and Montenegro only (return to Tirana, no Bosnia), avoids the one-way fee and Bosnia permission issue, removing about EUR 100 pp. Kosovo and Serbia not included. Replace Durmitor with Cetinje/Lovćen if snow closes roads.
