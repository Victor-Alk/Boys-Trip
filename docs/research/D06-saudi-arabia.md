# D06 — Saudi Arabia: research notes

Research date: 2026-11-27 (agent A06). Security classification: **EXCLUDED** (current official advice). The itinerary is
informational only; the budget is indicative.

## Overview

Concept: ancient Arabia — Nabataean and Dadanite/Lihyanite archaeology around AlUla (Hegra, Dadan, Jabal Ikmah),
sandstone canyons and rock towers, the Harrat volcanic fields (Khaybar, Uwayrid), the UNESCO rock art of the Hail
region (Jubbah, Shuwaymis), the Tuwaiq escarpment ("Edge of the World") and Diriyah. The cultural and geographical
case is very strong. The destination cannot currently be recommended because of the security situation (below).

## February vs March (climate normals, not a forecast)

AlUla / north-west plateau (approximate normals, estimate): February day 22–25 °C, night 6–9 °C; March day 26–29 °C,
night 9–12 °C. Riyadh/Najd: February day 20–24 °C, night 8–11 °C; March 24–29 °C, night 12–15 °C. Rain is rare but
flash floods occur in wadis (FCDO notes flooding risk November–February). February is cooler for hiking; March is
warmer with more wind/dust risk. Ramadan 2027 (≈ 8 Feb – 9 Mar, moon-sighting dependent): daytime eating, drinking
and smoking in public are not allowed; many restaurants shut or open only after sunset; tourist sites keep reduced or
shifted hours; Eid al-Fitr ≈ 9–10 March brings public holidays and domestic travel demand. A mid-to-late March window
(after Eid) would be the most practical, but is unusable while the security classification stands.

## Itinerary rationale (informational)

If conditions changed, a 12-day concept would be: fly CDG to Riyadh or Jeddah, domestic flight to AlUla; 5 days in
AlUla (Hegra, Dadan, Jabal Ikmah, Elephant Rock, Old Town, Harrat Uwayrid volcanic cones with a guide); drive/fly to Hail
(Jubbah and Shuwaymis rock art, Jabal Umm Sinman); a night in the Najd desert; Edge of the World day trip and Diriyah
from Riyadh. No operational day-by-day plan has been built because the route is excluded.

Reachability notes (not verified on official pages today): Hegra requires a booked guided tour with official site
transport. Jabal Ikmah and Dadan are accessed through the AlUla operator system. Harrat Khaybar and Jabal al-Qidr
are open-country areas where I could not confirm current access rules; treat as unverified. Tabuk/NEOM: FCDO
and France pages fetched do not give route-specific detail; NEOM construction zones have restricted access. Najran and
Asir (Abha, Khamis Mushait, Jizan) are excluded outright by France and by the Houthi attack history.

## Transport and driving legality

- Reported (secondary sources, conflicting): foreign or international licences allowed for visitors up to 1 year or the
  licence expiry (2025 reports), versus an older 3-month limit for IDP use. FCDO states a UK photocard is usable for the
  first 3 months and hire companies may require an IDP. Only two of the four travellers hold car licences; the others
  cannot drive. No motorcycle riding without the legal licence; alternatives: tours, hired driver, taxis/ride-hailing,
  domestic flights.
- Road safety is poor (FCDO); travel in daylight and convoy in desert areas. Fines are sent by SMS and must be paid before
  exit. Rental agreements often have daily km limits.
- Distances (approx.): AlUla–Hail about 400 km; Hail–Riyadh about 650 km; AlUla–Madinah about 350 km. Use domestic flights
  (Riyadh–AlUla) to avoid long driving, but airports are attack targets.

## Security assessment

Checked 2026-11-27:

- **France Diplomatie** (page updated 20 Nov 2026): formally advises against Najran, Abha, Khamis Mushait, Jizan (Asir/Jazan),
  against travelling to or transiting King Khalid (Riyadh) and King Fahd (Dammam) airports, and to avoid the Riyadh financial
  centre, the Al Shuqaiq power plant and Red Sea ports. The page text I retrieved gave no colour code; the interactive carte de
  vigilance was not read.
- **UK FCDO** (updated 18 Nov 2026): against all travel within 10 km of the Yemen border; against all but essential travel to
  the rest of Saudi Arabia. Houthi missile/drone attacks since 13 July; Riyadh airport struck 10 Oct 2026; further airport
  attacks "very likely"; Iran-related regional attack risk remains high. Terrorism threat: high. Travel insurance may be invalid
  if travelling against FCDO advice.
- **Netherlands Worldwide**: orange (travel only if necessary) for most of the country since 8 Oct 2026; red for 10 km along the
  Yemen border including Najran; Riyadh airport attacked several times since 10 Oct; Dammam airport attacked 21 Oct.
- **Al Jazeera and others (news)**: 10 Oct 2026 Houthi attack on Riyadh airport, 12–13 killed, 300+ wounded; follow-up strikes.
- **Smartraveller and US State Department**: pages could not be fetched (timeout / HTTP 403). A web search summary suggested a
  US Level 3 (reconsider travel) with Level 4 for some areas; this is unverified. No advisory level is asserted here.

Conclusion: the route depends on areas under "all but essential" advice and on airports under active attack, and cannot
be rerouted without leaving the country. Classified excluded.
