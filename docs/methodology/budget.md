# Budget methodology

Implemented once in `src/lib/budget.ts` (used by the website, the validator and the backend registry) and tested in `tests/unit/budget.test.ts`.

1. **Currency and group.** All amounts in EUR, per person unless stated. Group of four, two shared twin rooms where rooms apply.
2. **Lines.** Each line has a unit cost per scenario (economical / realistic / comfortable), a quantity (fixed or per scenario) and a sharing rule:
   - `individual` — every traveller pays unit × quantity (flights' baggage, visas, insurance, tickets…);
   - `shared` — the group pays unit × quantity, divided by 4 (vehicle, fuel, rooms, drivers, guides, boats…).
   `basis` (per-person / per-group / per-day / per-vehicle / per-room / per-activity) documents how the figure was built.
3. **Every category is present.** baggage, domestic flights, vehicle rental, vehicle insurance, fuel, tolls & parking, public transport, private drivers, accommodation, campsite fees, camping equipment, food, drinking water, entrance fees, activities, guides, visas, permits, travel insurance. Irrelevant categories are `not-applicable` (0); costs that could not be estimated are `unknown` (`null`) — **never 0** — and are listed on Slide 3 as excluded from the total.
4. **Contingency** is computed (normally 10–15 %) on the local subtotal only. It is never a line, so it cannot be double counted, and it does not move when a traveller enters a different flight price.
5. **Flights** are included in every headline. Reference departure: Paris CDG for every destination (illustrative — real departure airports are not decided). Each estimate records arrival/return airports, return vs open-jaw, indicative dates, airlines or source, connections, journey time, baggage, check date and confidence: `observed` (a quotation actually seen), `representative` (typical or historical fare from a cited source) or `estimate` (planning assumption). No live quotations were invented.
6. **Totals.** Local per person = known lines + contingency; total per person = flights + local; group totals = × 4; remaining budget below €2,000 and €2,500 is shown.
7. **Classification** (realistic total per person, flights included): < €1,000 very affordable · €1,000–1,499 affordable · €1,500–2,000 within preferred budget · €2,001–2,500 exceptional expenditure (justification required) · > €2,500 outside budget (cannot enter the eligible ranking).
8. **Material uncertainty.** A destination with `materialUncertainty: true` or an unknown realistic cost stays out of the definitive ranking until the cost is confirmed. The flag is set when **either** (a) an essential cost component without which the expedition cannot happen (a mandatory agency or escort package, an expedition boat, permits, visas) has no published price or quotation and its plausible range is wider than ±15 % of the local budget, **or** (b) such an unconfirmed component could plausibly move the realistic total across the €2,500 maximum. Ordinary planning estimates (hotels, food, fuel) do not trigger it. Destinations within €100 of the €2,500 cap but without unconfirmed essentials are shown with an amber "close to the maximum" warning instead.
9. **Personal flight prices.** On Slide 3 each participant can replace the reference fare with their own estimate (saved only on their device). Totals update instantly; the group ranking keeps the common reference so everyone compares like with like.
